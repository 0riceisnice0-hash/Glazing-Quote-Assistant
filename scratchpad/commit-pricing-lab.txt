AND A DEFECT IN THE MEASUREMENT ITSELF, handed to the lab as item one: collect() treats every copy
of a pricing file as a separate job, including - Copy.xlsx. Zelltec Crownhill appears four times,
three identical. The mean is therefore weighted by how many copies happen to sit in a folder, and
so are the learned rates, which is the worse half. You cannot measure 1-to-1 against a corpus that
counts some jobs three times.
