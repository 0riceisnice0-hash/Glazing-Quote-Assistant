The client's requirement, as drawn

Extract, drawings K1653-11 (first floor) and K1653-12 (second floor), Campbell Ark, CONSTRUCTION ISSUE. The identical note appears on both sheets.

Free area - requirement against quote

Required (drawings)1.00 m² free area
BasisPer stairwell. The note appears once per stairwell, at that stairwell's top storey.
Quoted (A Plus)1.30 m² geometric
PositionComplies on a geometric reading, with 0.30 m² to spare.
If aerodynamicA Plus's aerodynamic figures run 60-62% of geometric on the same product (QT51516, Towcester Vale: 0.49/0.81 and 0.54/0.87). On that ratio 1.30 m² geometric is roughly 0.78-0.81 m² aerodynamic - about 20% short of 1.00 m². Indicative only: different sizes, and a 900mm stroke against 850mm here.
The one question that decides this. "Free area of 1m²" is not qualified on the drawing. On a geometric reading the vents as quoted comply comfortably. On an aerodynamic reading they do not, and nor would A Plus's own proposed 1235 x 1583. A Plus must state the aerodynamic figure for the actual Riverside sizes, and the fire strategy must confirm which basis applies.

Locations - one vent per stairwell, at that stairwell's top storey

AOV.02 - Stairwell 2, FIRST FLOOR (top storey of this wing). Drawing K1653-11, note leader 3.

Excluded from this drawing and from the price

  1. The AOV control system in its entirety - smoke control panel, mains and battery-backed supply, cabling and containment from panel to vent, the fire brigade override at ground floor access level required by the note above, interfacing, commissioning and the O&M / EN 12101 documentation. A Plus fix the actuator, test it on local batteries and leave ~2m of flex coiled at the vent. The drawing's requirement cannot be met by the window alone.
  2. Window restrictors (note 4).
  3. Builder's work - forming, adapting or making good openings, lintels, internal finishes. Understood to sit with PHDB under the building works package.
  4. Access equipment / scaffold to top-storey level.
  5. Ongoing maintenance of the life-safety system, which is a legal duty of the occupier or agent under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005.
