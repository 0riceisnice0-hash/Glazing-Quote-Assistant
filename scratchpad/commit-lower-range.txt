Paul forwarded a Once For All addendum notice with no attachment. The zip was in
the archive folder: ERs Document.pdf, twenty pages of scan with no text layer.
pdfplumber returned nothing and the Read tool cannot open a PDF on this machine
because poppler is not installed, so I rendered it with PyMuPDF at 180dpi and read
the pages. There is no OCR available here and none was needed.

It is pages 32-51 of 51 of Section Nr.5, Project Specific Requirements, by b&m,
being exactly parts 2.13 and 2.14. I checked whether the missing 31 pages were an
error before calling it one. They are not: clarification C08 item 7 asked for the
Employer's Requirements and was answered "refer to Section 5 part 2.13", and this
is Ermine issuing precisely that. The reissued Document Register confirms nothing
else changed and the return date is still 07 August.

It unblocks the price. Clause 2.13.4.6.3 requires WER band A, B only where A is
genuinely not achievable, with the glazed unit at 1.2 W/m2K. That answers C22, the
question the handover recorded as the biggest cost driver in the package and
unresolved. It is a standard soft-coat argon warm-edge unit; the A rating is the
driver, not the make-up.

And the RFI I inherited did not need asking. Triage flagged the internal frame
face as unstated and was right to be careful after Georgie's, but was reading the
Materials Schedule, which is a planning-condition document rather than the spec.
The notes box on window schedules 25.578.27-30 says external dark grey, internal
white. The ER pins the external to RAL 7016 to match the rainwater goods. So it is
RAL7016 foil on white, the standard cheaper dual-colour, and the same string BSW
write as "Grey Foil On White (7016)". One RFI closed, and a rule posted to the
board: read the schedule notes box before raising any spec RFI.

What the ER adds to our cost was invisible until today. Secured by Design across
windows, doors and ironmongery. PAS 24 with external and front doors bought as
door sets. Outward opening reversible units above 1100mm. Child-proof stainless
friction stays. A sample window approved before fabrication starts, which binds
programme more than cost. External jamb mastic written into the windows clause
itself, where Gordon Court carried mastic as an option.

The two documents disagree on safety glass and the drawing is the wider one. The
ER wants laminated below 800mm; the schedules want BS EN 356 class P2A across all
ground floor windows and doors. I am pricing the drawing and saying so, because
2.14.1 makes a silent exclusion an undeclared variation the Employer can reject.

The Gordon Court AOV trap is on this job. The second floor staircore windows carry
"AUTOMATIC OPENING VENT (1.0 SQM)" in a description column and nowhere else. That
is REQ-22 again, where seven AOVs and the smoke shaft louvres went in as ordinary
windows and put GBP 10,055.76 at risk. Caught before the take-off this time. There
are also three louvred doors with insect mesh, two of which were added at revision
T2 in June, after tender issue, so anyone working from the original set loses them.

Trickle vents are required at 8,000mm2 equivalent area per room, which is large.
I checked whether MVHR displaced them rather than assuming either way: C11 confirms
MVHR is ventilation and heat recovery only, heating is combi and radiators, and the
vents stand.

REQ-31 raised for Adam. The ER demands a ten year insurance backed guarantee, which
is a third party product with a premium and not the house ten years the board
records as covering glass and frames. Behind it sit thirty years against rot, ten
against manufacturing defects, eight on the decoration and five on ironmongery.
Also flagged: the collateral warranty, and a DiPPA project bank account whose use
applies at all supply chain levels, so it reaches us and not just Ermine.

Still missing, but now named rather than referenced. The schedules say performance
is per Quinn Ross energy strategy P3250-ENE-01, January 2026 - that is the real
identity of the "Tender Addendum 1" both C22 and C08.1 deflected to, and the only
document left that could move the glass spec. Nobody has confirmed whether the new
b&m ER supersedes the Frankham ER Section 48.0 the schedules tell us to read them
with, and neither Frankham document is in the pack.

Fourteen plots, all 2B/3P, three floors, two staircores per floor, a plant room and
a pipe trench. Not yet priced. The take-off is the long pole with ten days to go,
and the schedules are vector CAD so every unit has to be read off a rendered
drawing by hand.
