Following my Filwood check half an hour ago - I went back and found the BSW correspondence behind the
quote. It hardens three of the findings and adds one. Updated workbook attached, 15 findings now.

First, credit where it is due: Gintare's RFQ of 23 July was right. She asked BSW for Aluprof or similar,
mill-finish spandrel panels, RAL 7035 to the ED-04 and ED-05 DOORS only, U 1.0, g 0.5-0.6, Rw 32 dB on
ED-06, LPS 1175 SR2 on ED-06, access control prep, BS 6262 and BS EN 12600, level thresholds and M4(2)
clear openings. Nothing was missed except the ventilation zone.

The problem is what came back.


1. BSW HAVE PUT A NON-COMPLIANCE IN WRITING AND OUR TENDER DOES NOT CARRY IT.

Their email, 24 July 06:43, in full:

"we have met the u- and g- and acoustic value for glazing only, as these area commercial thermally broken
shopfront products they are non rebated."

The drawing schedule's U 1.0 and Rw 32 dB are element requirements, not glass requirements. So BSW have
told us the elements do not meet the specified performance.

Our proposal renders that as "Ug values noted between 1.0-1.1 W/m2K where quoted". True of the glass,
silent on the element, silent on the non-rebated frames, silent on the acoustic.

That is the one caveat the supplier actually wrote down, and it is the one the tender leaves out. If
Stepnell accept on the basis of the proposal, we own the gap.


2. THE SR2 DOORSET WAS ASKED FOR AND NOT ANSWERED.

Gintare asked for it explicitly. BSW's reply covers u/g/acoustic values and panels, and says nothing about
LPS 1175 SR2 at all. Nor mill finish, nor thresholds, nor M4(2) - all four were asked for.

Silence to a direct written request is not compliance. That moves this from "not priced" to "requested,
ignored, and about to be sold to Stepnell anyway".


3. THE SOLID PANELS IN THE VENTILATION ZONE ARE CONFIRMED AT SOURCE.

Same email: "I have used flat aluminium panels everywhere glass was not indicated."

The root cause is upstream of BSW - the RFQ never mentioned the ventilation zone, the mesh or the louvre,
so they filled the band with sheet. Not their error.


4. ON THE g-VALUE, BE CAREFUL WHAT WE REPEAT.

BSW claim to have met g 0.5-0.6 in the glazing. Their own quote names no coating and states no g-value and
no Rw figure anywhere - and the make-up they list, 6.8 laminated / 4 toughened, sits around 0.7 clear.

I am not saying they are wrong. I am saying we cannot evidence it, and we should not pass their claim to
Stepnell until they put the coating and the number in writing.
