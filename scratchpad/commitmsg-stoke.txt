REQ-3 answered: the 46 panes are louvres - and both buys are sized wrong

Adam answered REQ-3 on the dashboard: the 46 "missing" panes are louvres, glazed
in in place of glass, from IKON rather than Vetroseal. He is right and the
shortfall finding is withdrawn.

Two documents were already in the job folder and neither had been opened when
REQ-3 was raised: Aplus panel order QT50932 Rev7 (a PANEL ORDER, 46 panels,
27.64 m2) and IKON quote Q26-24329 of 02/07 (46 IKL332 28mm louvre modules,
GBP 10,125.91 + carriage). They sit at ref A1 on every bay of Types A/B/C/D/F/G,
the head over D02 and D04, and all three panels of Door Type C / D03, which is a
louvred door. Remove them and the glass reconciles exactly - 124 required
against 124 quoted, +1.73 m2, about GBP 197. No shortfall and no GBP 2,920.

Running the same comparison on size rather than count found something worse.
Aplus re-input job 17644 on 02/07 and signed the frames off the same day; both
Fenster's buys predate it. Not one of the 124 quoted panes matches an ordered
size, and every one of the 41 window louvres is 85mm too tall against the
signed-off aperture. Frames land 03/08. Raised as REQ-11.

Also found: the cost build-up carries Vetroseal GBP 9,309.22 and IKON
GBP 7,490.64, which are the 05/06 quotes to the penny, against current quotes of
GBP 12,012.88 and GBP 10,125.91 - GBP 5,338.93 above the sold price on a job
already ordered. Moving the glass to CN Glass would roughly recover it.
