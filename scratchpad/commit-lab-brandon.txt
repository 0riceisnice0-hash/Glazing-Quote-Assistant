Brandon Estate 4.094 is the reader reading a working cell, and the block ends at the header

The last big open supplier question, GBP 1.12m of apparent gap on a GBP 3.17m
document, and it decomposes to the penny. The earlier Brandon revision NAMES BSW
and Vetroseal in its 'Supplier used:' block and types no cost against either. The
block window then ran six rows past the names into row 9 and took 362,678.40 out
of column 15 - a spare working cell holding that row's own (frames + glass) x qty:
686.80 + 176.72 = 863.52, x 420 = 362,678.40 exactly. Its sibling revision carries
the same working cell (470,076.60 = 1,119.23 x 420) and was never affected, because
its block has real figures in it.

BOTH GUARDS THAT SHOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT FAILED, AND FOR THE SAME REASON - they are
tolerances on a shape rather than facts about the template. Stop-at-first-empty-row
cannot fire on a block that is blank throughout. And looks_like() recognises a
priced row by its Additional cell holding a number, which is exactly the cell row 9
leaves empty. Replaced with the structural boundary: the priced-row header carries
'Qty' and 'Unit Rate', every supplier block in the archive sits above it, so nothing
at or below it can be part of the buy. Both older rules kept - a block that ends
early should still end early.

MEASURED BY RUNNING BOTH READERS OVER ALL 65 DOCUMENTS IN ONE PROCESS rather than
by trusting the finding count, because the count moving by one does not prove which
one moved. Exactly 1 of 65 documents changes and it is the one under investigation
(362678.4 -> None). scratchpad/sup_blockdiff.py loads the old read_doc out of git
HEAD alongside the new one; that is the cheap way to bound a reader change and is
worth reusing.

AND WHAT IS LEFT OF IT IS A REAL FINDING OF A NEW KIND: SUP_BLANK, a block that
names suppliers and states no cost. Three documents - the earlier Brandon
(1,484,932.13 of frames) and both Zelltec Crownhill UPVC revisions (13,521.20 and
36,202.66, and these are the 8.268 / 22.139 the board had filed only as a reader
false alarm). Not an arithmetic error, and nothing a client holds is wrong: it is a
control gap. With no buy recorded there is nothing to check the margin against, and
SUPPLIER, SUP_DUP and the whole-unit test all skip the document in silence. The old
reader could never report this, because whenever the block was blank it filled the
gap from whatever number the window reached.
