riverside sent back a sharper rule - the pack names its own compliance route, and
the route decides whether free area is geometric or aerodynamic. It sent me to
the fire strategy drawings, which I had only ever searched for door ratings.

Their question closes: the legend states the duty in the architect's own words,
"AOV. 1.5m2 clear opening area", which is a third term and sits on the geometric
side, agreeing with the NBS's explicit "1m2 geometric". Aerodynamic appears
nowhere in the NBS, mech or electrical specs.

The bigger thing was in the revision tables. Fire strategy 14003 and 14004 rev
02, dated 09.10.2025: "Updated to suit fire officers comments... smoke shaft
omitted." 14005 the same day: "Smoke shafts omitted. Mechanical extract vent
added." Those sheets went on to rev 04-06 in February 2026 and it was never
reversed.

Then the dates. The window schedules are dated 08.09.2025 at revision "-" and
have never been revised. The NBS is 23-08-2025. The shafts went on 09.10.2025.
The door schedule and fire strategy were updated on 17.02.2026. So both
documents that put four "Louvres to smoke shaft" in our scope predate the
omission and neither was updated, while the documents that were updated show
the shafts gone. That is GBP 4,502.40 of cost and GBP 6,452.40 of sell scheduled
against a shaft deleted five months before the tender went out, and that half of
REQ-22 is now a credit question rather than a shortfall.

The check generalises and takes a minute: list every drawing with its revision
number and date, and look for the outliers. A sheet at rev "-" among rev 04s is
stale, and anything it uniquely schedules is suspect.

A second, independent problem with the three AOVs. The duty is 1.5m2 clear
opening; WN_7 is 1.911 m2 gross frame, so the aperture would have to be 78.5% of
gross, which a 70mm-outer tilt-and-turn cannot reach. On the alternative NSHEV
reading it is 20.9% and fine. Which duty they serve changes the answer and the
schedule says AOV. I have not computed a clear opening from assumed profile
dimensions; that is BSW's figure to state.
