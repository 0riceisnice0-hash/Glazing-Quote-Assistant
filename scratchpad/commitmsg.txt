The AOV answer splits per unit, and wall-versus-roof is unresolved

riverside ran the new-versus-existing question on their own two vents and found
it split - one stairwell has no wall opening at all, the other already has three.
Running that against my own answer shows I was right but too coarse.

The tag says the opening is new. It does not say what the opening is cut into.
WN_7 at level 1 is a new opening in retained fabric - the first floor demolition
plan says "Retained wall to be assessed on site" and requires new brick slips as
part of the facade works - so enlarging it means lintels, cutting masonry and
making good, none of it priced. Levels 2 and 3 sit in the two added storeys where
size is genuinely free. So Adam's ruling applies cleanly to two of the three
AOVs, not all three. On a part-refurbishment a new opening in retained masonry
and one in new build are different jobs, and the demolition plan is the document
that tells them apart.

Their other question is live here and I cannot answer it. The NBS's 1.5m2 unit is
roof-mounted; the fire strategy legend states the duty as 1.5m2 clear opening
area, which is that roof figure; the smoke shafts that would have linked lower
lobbies to a roof vent were omitted in October and replaced by a mechanical
extract duct; and our three units are wall units in corridors tagged AOV. Which
duty they discharge is unknown and it decides everything - comfortable against
the 0.4m2 NSHEV, marginal and unprovable against 1.5m2, or redundant like the
four louvres if the extract has taken it. That needs the architect or the fire
engineer rather than a supplier, and it is now the biggest open item on the AOV
package.

Adopted their closing distinction on the aperture arithmetic: they were
reconciling a figure the supplier had stated, I was predicting an unstated one.
Reconciling is robust because the test is whether it hangs together; predicting
is not because the test is pass or fail. Use the arithmetic to understand what a
supplier has told you, not to decide whether they comply.

And one of their checks I could not run, logged as not run. The untagged-glazing
test is not executable on this pack by text extraction - only one of four
proposed elevations yields any window tags, so they live in the CAD graphics
layer and it needs the sheets rendered. I would rather record a check as not done
than report an answer I did not earn.
