Gordon Court: the client is jLiving, and our price is held 163 days longer than our cost

First full turn of the gordon-court chat. The tender was already in at
GBP 368,376.70; this audits it against the pack rather than re-pricing it.

The pack was in the zip again, as Georgie's warned. The loose folder held 28
drawings; the sibling zip held jLiving's ITT, the Form of Tender, the Energy
Statement, a 186-page NBS spec, the programme and the asbestos survey.

Two things came out of it that change the job. Chigwell are not the client -
they are a main contractor who bid to jLiving on 22/07, and jLiving do not
announce until 16/09 or award until mid-October, so the silence we were about
to chase is structural. And jLiving's Form of Tender holds our price open for
180 days, to 18/01/2027, while all five supplier quotes behind it are 30-day
and lapse in early August: GBP 201,086.70, 54.6% of the tender, unfixed for
about 163 days against a firm lump sum executed as a deed. Neither supplier's
price binds even inside its 30 days, by their own printed terms.

That is now a rule rather than a note, because it is the third instance in a
day after John North Hall's 90 days and St Mary's contract-start version:
check_quote_validity_against_commitment, with the founding fixture.

Also found: GBP 723.87 of omitted cost, including a BSW extras line nobody had
seen; the third fire door is type D_T, quoted 100mm taller than its opening, as
a double where the schedule shows a single leaf, in a store whose
external/internal cell is blank; 2no type D_X external doors priced nowhere;
trickle vents at half the required area; acoustic vents ticked on 26 of 40
windows and quoted by no one; and PAS 24 absent from all four BSW quotes.

Cleared and recorded so nobody re-opens them: install does cover the fire
doors, the other three schedules reconcile unit-for-unit, and the client-facing
workbook leaks no cost - the twin-file practice Filwood should have copied.
