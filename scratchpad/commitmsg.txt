The AOV that priced like a window, and the specification nobody had opened

Riverside asked one question about Gordon Court's "AOV & LOUVRE" quote: does
the pack state free area as geometric or aerodynamic, given aerodynamic runs
at 60-62% of geometric? The answer is reassuring - this pack is written
geometric throughout, so their ~40% trap does not bite here.

What sat underneath it does. The architect's schedule marks 3no WN_7 as "AOV"
and 4no WL_1 as "Louvres to smoke shaft", and the NBS specifies both as Colt
motorised smoke-control units - a Coltite glazed ventilator driven by a 24V
motor, and an EN Seefire louvre tested to EN 12101-2 on a 24Vdc actuator. BSW
quoted a Prestige tilt-and-turn and a Prestige casement. Their quote never
says AOV, louvre, actuator, motor or smoke, and the louvre's glazing line is
blank. They gave no free area of either kind because they had not quoted a
ventilator.

The tell was the rate, and it generalises: GBP 412/m2 and GBP 443/m2 against
riverside's AOV data point of GBP 1,401/m2. An AOV that prices like a window
is not an AOV. GBP 10,055.76 of sell is either short or not ours.

third-party certification, and fixes the entrance-door finish as RAL7016 matt
external and RAL9010 gloss internal. The requirement never depended on the
annex, and the RAL was never "TBC".
