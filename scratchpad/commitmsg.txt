We do offer secondary glazing, and the archive had a price after all

Adam called Storm and came back: there is secondary glazing on the job, and
"please bear in mind we do offer secondary glazing". So it is a capability rather
than something to refer out, and there is scope on Hammersmith as well as the
Maternity Assessment Unit.

I said yesterday there was nothing to benchmark it against. The rate register is
still empty on it - 80 categories, none - but the archive is not. Cranborne House
in Potters Bar, October 2025, holds a competitor's quote for the same work: five
windows at 2500 x 3600, 45.00 m2, GBP 17,420 ex VAT, which is GBP 387.11/m2 supply
and fit. Recorded with the caveats that matter more than the number - it is a
competitor's sell rather than our cost, the spec is timber subframes with magnetic
removable panels and no acoustic or thermal rating, the sizes came off a video
call, painting is excluded and weekend working is included. A hospital unit will
want aluminium-framed acoustic panels, so it is a floor for the cheapest solution
and not a rate.

Also checked and rejected the obvious false lead, because the filename will come up
in any future search: "OLICAT - St Thomas Secondary Glazing Quotation.pdf" is not
secondary glazing. Secondary is the school. The document contains no occurrence of
the phrase and prices replacement work including removal of old frames.

Steve has put the full Manor Lodge door spec to AFS - RAL 7021 30% gloss, external
half-cylinder linked to internal panic hardware, 600mm cranked handle, soft-touch
panic pad, 30 minute rating, fob entry and push to exit - and asked whether panic
gear is needed at all given the push-to-exit. That supersedes the 900 against
920mm push-bar problem, since a pad needs less leaf width than a bar. Handover row
updated; still watched rather than priced.

One thing picked up in passing that was not mine to handle but was worth
broadcasting. Adam's reply to Guildmore states the house position to a client:
strip out of old frames is allowed for, disposal and skips are not. That settles a
question St Mary's and Gordon Court have both been carrying. But John North Hall's
ITT expressly requires disposal and a waste carrier licence, so the standard
exclusion would contradict the client's own document there - flagged to that chat
rather than left to be applied blind.
