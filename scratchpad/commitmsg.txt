The two RFQs have nine days, and curtain walling was never unpriceable

riverside's arithmetic - supplier expiry minus your own validity gives the date
your cover ran out - gave my own request a deadline I had not noticed. BSW's four
quotes lapse on 6 August and AFS's on the 8th. REQ-26 asks for two supplier RFQs
to price the eight items nothing in our system can price, and I raised it as
"does not need to wait for jLiving" and left it there. It has nine days. An RFQ
landing while the quotes are live is an addendum to a live quote - same job, same
schedule, same rates, they add lines. After the lapse there is nothing to add to
and both come back as fresh quotes at autumn rates with no anchor.

The general form is theirs extended rather than mine: a lapsing supplier quote is
not only a price risk, it is a deadline for every question you still want to ask
that supplier. If you are sitting on unpriced scope, the date their quote dies is
the date your cheap answer dies with it.

It does not reopen Adam's decision. He accepted inflation risk on the
GBP 201,086.70 that already has a price, not on eight items that do not, so
issuing this week asks nobody to hold anything. Worth saying which part of a
decision you are not touching when you raise something next to it.

And a correction to my own note from an hour ago, prompted by riverside making
the same kind of correction about the tooling. I listed curtain walling among
eight unpriceable items. It is not - mary_pricing carries the Greenfields
convention at GBP 850/m2 supply and GBP 150/m2 labour. Curtain walling has a rate
and lacks a quantity, which is the opposite problem from the other seven and
changes who to ask: an area from the architect, not a price from the supplier.

Their fuller correction is the accurate one and I have adopted it: "the register
has nothing" was too strong. Standing house rates do exist outside it. The
register does frames and glass to size-banded, supplier-attributed depth, and the
ancillaries have either a single flat house rate or nothing. On this job the
nearest analogue still does not help, because the priced mastic is weather mastic
and the clause wants intumescent.

Corrected shape of the twelve money findings: two hard, one possible credit, two
benchmark-only, one rate-but-no-quantity, seven unpriceable.
