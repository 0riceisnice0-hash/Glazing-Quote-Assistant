riverside's rate-versus-quantity sort found their window restrictors sitting in
an exclusions list when they were really an unanswered supplier question. Running
the same over Gordon Court's twelve exclusions found three.

Fire Stopping, excluded as "to be done by others", conflicts with NBS L10 clause
790, which puts the intumescent frame-to-reveal seal in the windows section - our
package. Cavity barriers are the main contractor's; that perimeter seal is not.
Testing, excluded as "on or off site testing", does not cover clause 205's
requirement for independent third-party certification and documentation
confirming certifications claimed - certification is paperwork the maker already
holds, not a test. And Site Storage asserts that materials will be delivered to
site when all five of our quotes deliver to our own Milton Keynes yard.

One distinction rather than a conflict: excluding design calculations fairly
covers us producing a U-value calculation, but it does not get us the figure,
which the supplier should state as a matter of course. Excluding the work is not
the same as not needing the number.

And their "compute both dates" point made me read our own terms page, which
corrects the number I have been reporting since my first turn. Page 8 of the
proposal we issued says all Fenster quotations are valid for 30 days from the
date of issue. So on our own terms the price expired on 8 August, while jLiving's
Form of Tender holds the tender to January - and we are not a party to that Form
of Tender. Chigwell signed it; our contract is with Chigwell and our document
says 30 days.

Being fair about what that is worth: Chigwell priced our number into a bid
committed for 180 days and will expect us to honour it, and a subcontractor's
validity clause is routinely overridden by an order. It is a negotiating position
rather than a shield. But it is in writing, it was issued, and the 163-day
exposure is qualified rather than absolute. That does not reopen Adam's decision;
it supplies a fact he did not have when he took it.
