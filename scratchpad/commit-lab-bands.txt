The size-band question is closed and the answer is no - the adder already does it

A clean negative, recorded so the next session does not spend the hour. The
question carried in was: St Mary's whole-job error was +4.4% but its register
bands were -35.5% (<1.5m2), -1.2%, +37.5% and +35.2% (>6m2), so small runs low
and large runs high and the bands cancel - and if that is real across jobs then
the band structure is wrong and fixing it beats any number of individual rates.

It is not real. Measured two independent ways:

  HONEST PER-LINE ERROR BY AREA, all 508 lines, three folds, every line scored
  on rates mined from other jobs: 0-1m2 +3.7%, 1-1.5 -1.6%, 1.5-2 +5.1%, 2-3
  +1.2%, 3-5 +3.2%, 5-6 +30.4% (n=7), 6+ +0.9%. Flat, and not monotonic. By the
  engine's own learned bands: <2m2 +3.0%, 2-5m2 +2.1%, >5m2 +3.8% - uniform to
  within 1.7 points.

  ST MARY'S NO LONGER SHOWS THE SPREAD: -14.2 / +0.2 / -6.3 / +4.0, whole job
  -3.7%. The whole-job sign has flipped since 29/07 too, so the engine has moved
  underneath the figure - but the claim that mattered is simply not there.

WHY NOT, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING. Within a single product code the
estimator's raw GBP/m2 really does fall steeply with area - MAW runs 1,149 / 410
/ 385 / 309 across the buckets, SAD 954 / 556 / 378, LAW 950 / 420 / 337. That is
why the hypothesis was plausible. But a declining GBP/m2 is the signature of a
fixed cost per opening plus a variable cost per m2, and the template already
charges a fixed cost per opening: the code adder, 0.75 x code value, identical
whatever the size. On a small SAW that adder is GBP 337.50 against about GBP 322
of supply, so more than half the unit rate is already a flat per-unit charge. The
bands only ever had to carry the residual variable part and three of them do it.
The engine already has the structure the question was asking for. It is in the
adder, not the bands.

AND AN AGGREGATE BAND ERROR IS A MIX EFFECT. St Mary's "<1.5m2 band" was 41 lines
of one product and its "3-6m2 band" was 3 lines of another, so the two bands were
really two products and the spread was measuring product mix. Re-measure any
per-job band pattern across the whole archive before treating it as structural.

ONE REAL RESIDUAL, NOTED NOT FIXED. The 5-6m2 bucket is +30.4%, the worst in the
archive, on 7 lines - four of them the Zelltec Crownhill DADs at 5.24m2. They sit
inside the >5m2 learned band, which wants about GBP 362/m2 at 5-6m2 and more
above 6m2. Seven lines will not support a band of their own with MIN_LEARNED_N at
6, and splitting bands finer was already tested on 29/07 and was worse.

No engine change in this commit. Board and calibration record only.
