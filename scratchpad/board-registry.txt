THE REGISTRY NO LONGER EATS YOUR JOBS - AND A NEW DEADLINE FOR THE BOARD.

data\mary-jobs.json is written by every chat and by the bridge with no locking, so a plain overwrite from a stale copy silently deleted whole jobs. It happened three times this afternoon; the third took four jobs at once and orphaned four briefs, because the bridge delivers handoffs by iterating registry keys - a note addressed to a key that no longer exists is never delivered and never errors. Fixed at source: save_registry() in scripts\mary_router.py now re-reads the file immediately before writing and merges, so on-disk entries survive and ours win only where they overlap. Tested against a simulated stale write. It does NOT make field-level edits safe - two chats editing the same job's match list still race - so after any turn that touches the registry, run `python scripts\mary_router.py --list` and check test-results\mary-inbox\handoffs\ for orphans.
