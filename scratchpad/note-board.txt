THE WINDOW SCHEDULE IS NOT THE ONLY PLACE A U-VALUE IS SET. NEW CHECK, RUNS ON EVERY PUBLIC-SECTOR JOB.

On St Mary's the architect's schedule 2376-09 says "achieve u value of 1.4 w/m2k" against every window
type, 33 times. We priced 1.4 and promised 1.4. But EDG02 "Energy and Carbon Design Guidelines -
Building Fabric", sitting in section 7.05 of the SAME tender pack, sets the client's minimum for the
REFURBISHMENT column at 1.3 W/m2K windows, 1.2 W/m2K external doors, and a glazing g-value of 0.4-0.3.
Our number misses both. The energy annex is filed under sustainability, nobody opens it, and it is the
tighter requirement. On any council / education pack, open the energy or carbon appendix and read its
performance table before accepting the figure on the window schedule - and note it usually splits
WINDOWS from EXTERNAL DOORS, which the schedule rarely does.

The g-value line matters as much as the U-value. 0.4-0.3 means solar control. Neither of our suppliers
quoted any: zero hits for solar, g-value, Suncool, SKN, Coolite or Planitherm across both quotes. "Clr"
means clear.

AND THE GEORGIE'S RULE HELD AGAIN, ON THE DEARER HALF OF THE JOB. BSW's Sheerline quote at least names
the glass ("6.8 Lam/18/4mm Clr Tuff EcoPlus 1.0" - a centre-pane Ug, not a whole-window Uw). Bellview's
SMA quote for the doors and the MC600 curtain walling - GBP 30,352.38, 33% of our cost - states NO
U-value, NO low-E, NO soft coat, NO argon, NO warm edge and NO coating of any kind. Just "6.8 Lami /
4mm Tuff". Aplus put the default in writing on their own advisory notes: "Commercial doors and framing
will be supplied with a U-Value of up to 3.0 W/m2/K". Doors and curtain walling are where the thermal
silence lives - check them separately from the windows.

TWO MORE FROM THE SAME JOB, both worth running elsewhere:

1. SUPPLIER QUOTES CAN EXPIRE BEFORE THE JOB EVEN STARTS. St Mary's: BSW valid to ~14/08, Bellview to
   ~15/08, JCT MW start on site 14/09 and completion 11/12 with GBP 500/day delay damages. So
   GBP 91,409 of cost is unfixed before day one against a fixed sell. Compare quote validity against the
   CONTRACT START DATE in the SOW, not against the tender return date - they are months apart.

2. THE CLIENT'S SCHEDULE OF WORKS CAN PUT STRIP-OUT INSIDE YOUR ITEM. MTCBC's SOW item 1.09 reads
   "Remove doors and windows; load into skip; existing window structures and prepare opening to receive
   new (ALLOWED IN 6.01)" - and 6.01 is the windows-and-doors supply-and-fit line, i.e. ours. Our
   proposal excludes "waste removal" generally and never names removal of the existing windows. On 107
   openings that is not a rounding error. Read the demolition/strip-out section of a SOW for
   cross-references into your own item number.

SEARCH TIP THAT COST ME A FALSE ALARM: BSW/Bellview write obscure glazing as "ObsTuff" (e.g. "6.8
Lam/18/4mm ObsTuff EcoPlus 1.0 Stippolyte 4mm"). Grepping a BSW quote for "obscure" returns zero and
looks like a missing spec item. It was there all along - 9 panes. Search for Obs, Stippolyte, Satin or
Pattern before raising it.
