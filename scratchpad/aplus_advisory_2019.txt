responsibility of the Customer to undertake full structural calculations on all brackets/spigots supplied by A Plus.

ventilation / safety.

Product Performance
• It is the responsibility of the Customer to ensure all building regulations (i.e. Part ‘B’, 'F', 'L', 'M' & 'N' and any others relevant to the building), Life Time Homes,
Secured by Design, PAS 24 (formally BS 7950) are adhered to. The Supplier does not warrant or represent that any Product supplied shall comply with any of
the aforementioned standards unless where expressly stated to the contrary by the Supplier.
• Unless otherwise stated, windows and doors will have a U - Value no better than 1.8. Commercial doors and framing will be supplied with a U-Value of up to 3.0
Wm2/K (depending on specification of product).
• All residential windows to have a minimum window energy rating of C. For unglazed frames U-Value and WER ratings are dependent on glazing type used and A
Plus should be contacted to confirm the thermal performance of the frames.
Finishes
• Frames to be a single colour (same internal and external) polyester powder coated finish, the colour of which is to be from our standard range of Interpon or
Syntha Pulvin RAL colours, unless indicated otherwise on the quotation above.
• Depending on the product and supplier, the polyamide thermal break may have the powder coat finish applied to it. No warranty is extended on the adhesion of
the powder coat to the polyamide. Should this not be required, then the profile will have to be assembled after coating which may incur additional cost and lead
time. Please check prior to order should this be the case.
Hardware
• Unless otherwise stated we allow in our estimates for standard ironmongery as recommended by our systems suppliers and compatible with their section. Any
non-standard items requested and included in our price may affect our programme and nullify any system or performance warranty on that product.
• Reynaers and Metal Technology Tilt before Turn gearing uses visible hinges as standard – please ensure this is acceptable for your installation. If concealed
gearing or deeper frame profiles are required please inform us as it may incur price and lead time changes.
Glazing
• Unless indicated otherwise all frames are delivered unglazed.
• Non-Tilt Vertical Sliders will have the top sash (only) glazed to ease site installation and this is included in our quoted rates. All other sliding windows are either
unglazed or supplied with loose glass according to the quote. When requested each additional glazed sash will be charged at £20 each.
• Arched head windows will be glazed at a cost of £20 each above the top transom line to avoid having to remove curved beads on site.
Frames
• Sliding & Bi-fold doors will be delivered with outer frames in kit form to avoid joint movement whilst handling on site.
• Window frames over 4 metres in length will be split down and coupled for ease of handling, transport and to avoid joint movement.
Curtain Walling
• We allow for top and bottom spigots to suit relevant mullion dimension. Spiggots are supplied loose in 300mm lengths ready for screw fixing to a pre-drilled 8mm
aluminium plate (max 250mm x 250mm). Other dimensions or specifications are subject to additional cost.
• Sides and head of curtain wall screens have an extruded pvc rebate closer included.
Delivery
• All orders are priced as Ex-Works.
• Loads over £5000 + VAT will be delivered FOC within a 50-mile radius of Watford
• Where loads are less than £5000 + VAT in value, A Plus will batch customer orders to deliver in the most efficient manner possible each day to alleviate additional
charges.
• Where this is not possible, or the customer requests delivery on a specific day which does not allow efficient batching as above, single customer deliveries with a
value less than £5000 + VAT will be charged at £1 / mile each way from Watford or previous drop, depending which is lower.
• Deliveries are made on our own vehicles (either Cargo or Luton box vans with rear doors). We require suitable labour at the delivery point to unload.
• Where the client is not able to take delivery of the product on the agreed dates, A Plus may need to store the product elsewhere. Costs for storage and re-delivery
will be charged.
Stillages:
• Where stillage deliveries are required, deliveries are made by independent haulier using flat-bed vehicles as required. These loads are priced according to
location and you will be advised of charges prior to delivery.
• An additional £400 will be charged for each load to cover the cost of collecting empty stillages from site once used. Where we are able to bring back stillages on a
return vehicle after a site delivery, A Plus will credit that particular charge.
End.
