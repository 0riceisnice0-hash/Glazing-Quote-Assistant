WHY IT DOES NOT MOVE OUR NUMBER

I compared the schedule we priced (2376-09) against rev A attribute by attribute rather than eyeballing it. Everything that drives price is identical: 209 window references, 38 window types, 28 structural opening sizes, 38 opening patterns, 24 restrictor notes, 6 obscured glazing notes, 33 U-value notes, 38 Secured by Design notes. Not one change.

The single change is the note "Magnetic operated integral blinds. Colour: Silver", which drops from 29 appearances to 1.

We never priced blinds. They are excluded in black and white on page 3 of our proposal - "magnetic integral blinds" sits in the exclusions list, and the clarifications say "any integral blind requirements are to be confirmed prior to manufacture". Neither BSW QT252799 nor Aplus QP70172 contains a blind. So the client has removed something we had already carved out. If anything our position improves - the exclusion can no longer be argued as a gap.
