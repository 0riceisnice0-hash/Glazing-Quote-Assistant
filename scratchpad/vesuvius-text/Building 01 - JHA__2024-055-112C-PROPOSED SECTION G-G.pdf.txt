NOTES:
Air Permeability:
All junctions, interfaces, penetrations, service entries and construction
details are to be installed and finished to maintain the continuity of the
airtightness line.
• Vapour control layers (VCLs) and airtight membranes to be
lapped, bonded, and sealed in strict accordance with all
manufacturer’s recommendations.
• All junctions between membranes and adjoining elements (e.g.
blockwork, steelwork, window/door frames) to be sealed with
proprietary air-tightness tapes and compatible primers.
• Service penetrations (mechanical, electrical, plumbing,
ventilation) and truss penetrations to be individually sealed
with airtight gaskets, collars, or tapes; no unsealed
penetrations are permitted.
• Damaged or punctured membranes to be repaired
immediately using proprietary air-tightness patches.
• The airtight layer must remain continuous around the entire
thermal envelope, from foundations to roof, with special
attention to wall–roof junctions around structural deck, wall–
floor junctions, and openings.
• Installation is to be coordinated with M&E to ensure
compliance with the SBEM/Part L air permeability target. • The contractor to ensure all works are completed in such a manner that the building achieves the specified air permeability test result at completion.
