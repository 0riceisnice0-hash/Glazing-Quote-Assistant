NOTES:
Air Permeability:
All junctions, interfaces, penetrations, service entries and construction
details are to be installed and finished to maintain the continuity of the
airtightness line.
• Vapour control layers (VCLs) and airtight membranes to be
Fire Stopping Partition Wall Detail Fire Stopping Partition Wall Elevation
lapped, bonded, and sealed in strict accordance with all
manufacturer’s recommendations.
1 : 10 1 : 10 • All junctions between membranes and adjoining elements (e.g.
blockwork, steelwork, window/door frames) to be sealed with
proprietary air-tightness tapes and compatible primers.
• Service penetrations (mechanical, electrical, plumbing,
ventilation) and truss penetrations to be individually sealed
with airtight gaskets, collars, or tapes; no unsealed
penetrations are permitted.
• Damaged or punctured membranes to be repaired
immediately using proprietary air-tightness patches.
• The airtight layer must remain continuous around the entire
thermal envelope, from foundations to roof, with special
attention to wall–roof junctions around structural deck, wall–
floor junctions, and openings.
• Installation is to be coordinated with M&E to ensure
compliance with the SBEM/Part L air permeability target.
• The contractor to ensure all works are completed in such a
manner that the building achieves the specified air
permeability test result at completion.
FOUNDATIONS:
Trench fill foundations taken down in GEN 3 mass concrete to a
minimum of 1000mm depth below existing ground level (to give S.G.B.P.
of 100kN/m².) Refer to JRD Design & Calcs.
DAMP PROOF MEMBRANE:
2000 gauge polyethylene damp proof membrane (or approved
equivalent) with taped and sealed joints, laid over sand blinding beneath
the reinforced concrete slab in accordance with the recommendations of
the G&M Consulting Ground Investigation Report.
No ground gas or radon protection measures are identified within
the Ground Investigation Report. Should any evidence of
contamination, unusual ground conditions, landfill materials,
hydrocarbon odours, staining, or other potentially contaminated
material be encountered during construction works, contractor is to
cease works immediately and JHA Architecture to be notified
without delay for instruction.
