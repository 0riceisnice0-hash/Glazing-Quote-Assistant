WALLS:
EXTERNAL:
Wall to comprise: 100mm 7N/mm² blockwork inner and outer leaf as
specified by structural engineer with 100mm cavity filled with 90mm thick
Kingspan Kooltherm K106 rigid insulation (10mm residual cavity). Type 2
Stainless steel cavity wall ties to be spaced at 750mm centers horizontally
& 450mm vertically & staggered with ties at 225mm vertical centers to
jambs of openings. Cladding to comprise kingspan helping hand bracket system (with thermal break pads) and angled / 'tee' vertical rails (refer to
details) with horizontal fixing rails. Dri-Design Cassette Rainscreen Facade
System fixed to rails as details with hidden fixings. All flashings to be
installed as manufacturer instructions to dimensions set out on JHA
drawings.
INTERNAL:
Wall to comprise: 100mm 7N/mm² blockwork as specified by structural
engineer with lining either side - refer to wall plan.
Wall to comprise: 75mm channel with gypframe 48'S'50'C' studs fixed at
600mm centres with lining either side - refer to wall plan.
ROOF :
Roof to be constructed of 0.9mm PVF2 Coated Aluminium Kalzip 65/400 -
Colour: RAL7000 with Kalzip Insulation Plus 40 - 280mm compressed to
265mm. Cladding supported on Kalzip E180 halter clips c/w 10mm &
5mm spacers supported on thermal pads fixed on 70mm deep
Continuous Galvanised Steel Top-Hat Cleader Rails. Rails sat on Kalzip
Clear Vapour Control Layer (VCL) fully lapped and sealed to ensure
watertight roof. All sat on 18mm WBP plywood deck on Truss (designed
by Tailor made design) all cantilevered as manufacturers design.
Roof to be underdrawn with MF Ceiling - Gypframe Primary Support
channels supported from Gypframe Strap Hangers.
ROOF TRUSS:
Roof truss' to be T1, T2 & T4 with suspended & cantilevered members all
to Tailor Made Design - Refer to drawing 2024-055-107.
FLOOR :
Floor to be constructed of floor finish - refer to finishes schedule on 90mm
floor screed on 500 micron polyethylene separating layer on 80mm
Kingspan Kooltherm K103 sat on 150mm RC35 concrete with 1No. layer
A193 mesh top on 2000 gauge damp proof membrane (fully lapped and
sealed) (No ground contamination), on 50mm sand blinding, on minimum
150mm clean dry hardcore Type 1 deposited and compacted in max
150mm layers. All to achieve 0.16W/m2k.
LINTELS:
Openings to external walls to have lintels as specified by structural
engineer, with tray DPCs over. Weep hole ducts at 450mm c/c and
minimum of 2no. per opening. Openings to internal walls to have Naylor
R6 concrete lintels or similar approved. All proprietary lintels fixed in
accordance with manufacturers instructions, length appropriate for span
including minimum 150mm end bearings. Steelwork to Structural
Engineer's design and specification.
WINDOWS AND DOORS:
Curtain Wall double glazed panel to achieve U-value of 1.2 W/m2/k.
Windows to be fitted with 4000mm2/ trickle vents. Apply sealant to front
and back of frame as shown. Refer to specialist design.
Double glazed window unit panels (Senior PURe® No Profile Groove
Windows) to achieve U-value of 1.2 W/m2/k. Windows to be fitted with
4000mm2/ trickle vents. Apply sealant to front and back of frame as
shown. All aluminium window frames to be thermally broken and to
continue the thermal line. All opening elements of windows & doors to be
sealed with draught excluder. Window and door frames to overlap the
closer by minimum 30mm. Apply sealant to front and back of frames/cills.
All internal door sets to be fire rated inline with fire plan.
