65 Hardcore filling
1. Fill: Granular material, free from excessive dust, well graded, all pieces less than 75 mm in any

2. Permitted materials in any one layer.
2.1. Test requirements
2.1.1. Minimum 10% fines value tested in a soaked condition to BS 812-111: Not required

2.2. Permitted materials in any one layer
2.2.1. Crushed rock (other than argillaceous rock) or quarry waste with not more binding

2.2.2. Crushed concrete, crushed brick or tile, free from plaster, timber and metal.
2.2.3. Crushed non-expansive slag.
2.2.4. Gravel or hoggin with not more clay content than is required to bind the material

2.2.5. Well-burned non-plastic colliery shale.
2.2.6. Natural gravel.
2.3. Natural sand.
3. Filling: Spread and level in 150 mm maximum layers. Thoroughly compact each layer.

sieve. In each layer only one of the following:
1.1. Crushed hard rock.
1.2. Crushed concrete, crushed brick or tile, free from plaster, timber and metal.
1.3. Gravel.
2. Filling: Spread and level in 150 mm maximum layers. Thoroughly compact each layer, whilst
maintaining enough voids to allow efficient venting.
