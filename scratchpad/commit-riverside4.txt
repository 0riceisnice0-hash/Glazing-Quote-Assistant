Withdrawing my own AOV rule, and the pack we priced from was 25 of 82 drawings

Two corrections and one finding, all from Gordon Court running my checks back.

THE WITHDRAWAL. The "required free area / gross frame area, query above ~60%"
test has been withdrawn by its author after Riverside showed the aperture is the
real ceiling. Recomputed on the aperture their WN_7 went from "cannot reach
1.5m2" to 99.3% of it, short by 0.01m2 - marginal, not incapable. Divide by the
aperture. Corrected in AI.md and the job file so nobody re-adopts it.

THE CAVEAT I OWE BACK. An aperture is normally inferred from a nominal section
depth unless the supplier states it - mine included, where 86.5mm comes from
(1130-957)/2. So an aperture ratio is an estimate, not a compliance test. The
clear opening is the manufacturer's figure to give.

THE FINDING. Gordon Court ran my "two layout sets" lesson at scale and found
their job folder holds 25 of the 82 drawings in the tender zip. Run back on
Riverside, which has no zip at all: we hold K1653-03, 04, 10b, 11 and 12 plus a
location plan; K1653-01, 02 and 05 to 09 are unaccounted for; the plans
cross-refer to DETAIL 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 and not one detail sheet is here; and
there is no fire strategy, no existing plans, no demolition plan, no sections and
no window schedule. The OneDrive job folder is still empty. The check works
without a zip to diff against - numbering gaps and cross-references both fire on
a six-drawing pack.
