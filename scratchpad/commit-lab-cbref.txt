The fourth and last of the open supplier questions, and like the other three it
decomposes exactly rather than needing a tolerance. The block is three figures and
two of them are the document's own rows to the penny:

    rows  9-13  aluminium windows      4,430.28  =  BSW         4,430.28   EXACT
    rows 16-21  steel doors           28,027.93  =  Strongdoor 28,027.93   EXACT
    row  14     curtain walling       30,073.00  v  BSW CW     15,994.08   gap +14,078.92
                document gap 62,531.21 - 48,452.29           =     +14,078.92

So the whole gap is one curtain-walling row, and BSW's own quote says the row is
right: Bellview 0000000371 prices 2 Pcs 2,900 x 6,100 Curtain Wall Elements at
9,996.30, Net 19,992.60, 'Discount 2: -20.00%', Grand Total Net GBP 15,994.08. The
quantity is right - the supplier quotes 2 and we price 2 - and the block figure is
right. QT250448 is the aluminium quote and its five items are 977.77 / 477.57 /
571.57 / 1,164.45 / 1,238.92, which is rows 9-13 in order.

WHAT IS WRONG IS THE FRAMES CELL ON ROW 14. It holds 15,036.50, which is 17.69m2 x
GBP 850 - the CW NOTIONAL, the same figure as the unit rate, copied across. The real
cost is 15,994.08 / 2 = 7,997.04. THE CONFIRMATION IS NOT THE ARITHMETIC, IT IS THE
OTHER SEVEN CW ROWS: on all of them Frames is a genuine lower figure sitting around
half the sell (St Mary's 4,419.91 / 8,655.98, Wisley 12,260.79 / 19,975.00, median
ratio 0.51). This row's ratio is exactly 1.000, which no other CW row shows - and
7,997.04 would put it at 0.532, square in the middle of the seven.

NOT AN ERROR IN ANYTHING A CLIENT HOLDS, so Adam's morning list is still Gordon
Court REV 2 alone. The document foots, the quantity is right, and the client is
charged correctly. It understates OUR OWN margin by 14,078.92 on this job - the row
reads as zero margin on 30,073.00 of sell.

AND IT DOES NOT COME OUT OF THE CALIBRATION SET, which is a more careful reading of
the lab's own rule than excluding it would be. The rule is never to tune the engine
to reproduce a defect. The defect here is in the COST cell, and the engine stopped
reading CW rows' cost cells in the previous commit - supply_money() returns None for
them, verified by the rate table being byte-identical. The SELL cell is correct
(area x 850, matching six other rows), so scoring it is sound. Exclude the cell, not
the document.
