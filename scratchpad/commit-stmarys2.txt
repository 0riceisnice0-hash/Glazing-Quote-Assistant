SMA publish 1.8 for their doors - we promised 1.4 and the client asked 1.2

Second turn on St Mary's. No work order for the job; advanced the open items instead
of waiting on humans.

Started from our own SM5 Wexham record - Smart Wall Pocket doors could not meet 1.6,
non-thermally-broken - and established the same system is on this job against a tighter
number. Bellview positions 001-006 are Smart Wall Pocket: 6 door types, 7 units,
22.078 m2, GBP 31,360.15 of sell, against EDG02's 1.2 W/m2K for external doors. Position
007 is MC600 Plus, thermally broken, the Type AK curtain walling - the system SM5 Wexham
named as part of the fix. The two must not be lumped together.

Then SMA's own datasheet turned up and made it concrete. It publishes 1.8 W/m2K for Smart
Wall doors and 1.4 for screens. Our proposal promises 1.4 across the package; the window
schedule asks 1.4; EDG02 asks 1.2. At 1.8 the doors miss all three - so the door U-value
fails under either reading of the pack and no longer waits on the EDG02-vs-schedule
question we have out with the client. That still decides the windows and the g-value.

The sheet reached us by accident, attached to an unrelated High Wycombe enquiry, and is
the only copy in the business. It was not in the job folder, not in the supplier's quote,
and nobody had asked for it. Stoke Park's lesson again: when a supplier will not state a
performance figure, check whether their own literature is sitting elsewhere in the system.
Caveats recorded - the sheet says "Smart Wall", never "Smart Wall POCKET", and 1.8 assumes
a proper unit where Bellview name no coating, no warm edge and no gas fill. In our favour
it certifies LPS 1175 Level 2 / BS EN 1627 Level 3, the first evidence the schedule's 38
Secured by Design notes are satisfiable on the doors.

Also quantified the g-value uplift rather than leaving REQ-15 as a question. The rate
register carries matched "incl solar control" categories against plain ones, same supplier,
same product, same band: +GBP 43.37/m2 median across 10 pairs, or GBP 16,489 band-matched to
our actual units. Corroborates Filwood's GBP 45/m2. Quoted as a range with its caveats - the
big bands rest on 39 and 9 solar lines, two pairs go negative on one line each, 23% of the
area has no matched pair, and it buys the g-value only, not a compliant door.

New check rule check_system_performance, fixture _test-st-marys.json. A system can be
fabricable and still be incapable of what the spec demands - check_fabricator_can_make_it
passes this job happily. capable:false FAILS, capable:null ASKs, because on both founding
jobs the supplier's answer already existed and nobody had gone and got it. Selftest passes,
all six founding errors still fire. Live manifest now 4 FAIL + 1 ASK.

Confirmed the registry regression for REQ-18: triage re-added five jobs at 17:32 and this
session's start erased all five again, orphaning the same briefs a second time. Deterministic,
not a race - only keys predating the bridge boot survive. Told triage not to re-add again.

A new enquiry arrived mid-turn and was left in the queue for triage - John North Hall,
High Wycombe, 5 blocks of communal entrance doors, due 9am 24 August. Not this job and not
named in the kick prompt. Checked one thing first so the handoff carried a fact: its ITT
sets no thermal requirement at all, so the Smart Wall finding does not bite there.
