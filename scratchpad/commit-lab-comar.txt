The COMAR re-price kept the Elkins component cells - and dropping them changes nothing

Closes the last BUILDUP class. All 11 remaining findings are in the COMAR Brandon
variant and the cause is exact: 11 of 11 non-footing rows have frames and glass
IDENTICAL TO THE PENNY to a row of the Elkins revision, while the COMAR variant's own
product codes were changed (every Elkins row is SAD; COMAR recodes them DAD, ELAW, MAW
and LAW) and so were its unit rates. The implied adders come out between 292.71 and
1,237.24 against code adders of 412.50 to 1,500.00, so those unit rates were never
built by the template's frames + glass + additional + adder formula at all. The
component cells are stale - left behind from the revision the file was saved from,
which is the same failure mode as the hand-typed 'Supplier used' cell and now the
second place it has shown up.

NOT AN ARITHMETIC ERROR. The document foots, TOTAL* is right, and it is an internal
DO NOT SEND working. Adam's morning list is still Gordon Court REV 2 alone. The other
2 of the 13 BUILDUP findings are the ASHE mis-coding already on the board.

AND THE ENGINE CONSEQUENCE IS A CLEAN NEGATIVE, which is worth recording precisely
because the argument for it is sound. supply_money() mines those very cells, so on 13
of 516 lines the engine learns one revision's supply money against another revision's
unit rate - a defect, and the lab's rule says never to learn from one. Measured
leave-one-JOB-out with mining filtered on whether the build-up foots to within GBP 1:
    A  mine every line (CURRENT)      12.28%   median 6.86%   bias -1.83%   17/29
    B  exclude non-footing lines      12.32%   median 7.23%   bias -1.89%   18/29
Better on 7 documents, worse on 9. Mean and median both move the wrong way and only
within-10 improves; the declared decision metric is the mean, and it was not changed
to rescue the result.

THE LESSON IS THE ONE THE DEDUP ALREADY TAUGHT, now confirmed from the other
direction: a median over 500 lines does not notice 13 of them. The corpus is robust to
a handful of bad rows, so there is no accuracy to be won by hunting them. Hunt them
because they are findings about our own documents - which is the other half of this
window's job, and the half where this one counts.
