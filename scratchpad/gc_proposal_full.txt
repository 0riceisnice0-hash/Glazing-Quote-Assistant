Executive Summary
Fenster Glazing are pleased to submit our proposal for the window and external door package at Gordon
Court, Stonegrove, Edgware, HA8 7TQ. The works form part of a residential extension and refurbishment
scheme to the existing building.
Our proposal is based on the manufacture, supply and installation of replacement windows, new
windows, patio / green roof doors and associated external / communal door elements where included
within Fenster’s scope. The schedules identify 40no. replacement windows, 84no. new window / louvre
items and 44no. patio / roof doors, with PAS 24, restrictors, trickle ventilation, acoustic requirements and
safety glazing noted where applicable.
The tender information includes a mixture of window and door requirements, including frosted glazing,
AOV windows, smoke shaft louvres and communal entrance door elements. The schedules and
specification include conflicting references to PVC-U and aluminium window systems, so the final
system/material basis should be confirmed prior to order.
With experience delivering glazing packages for residential refurbishment schemes, Fenster Glazing
understand the importance of accurate site measurement, coordination with existing openings and early
clarification of specification discrepancies. We will work collaboratively with the project team to support
a compliant and well-coordinated glazing package.
For a full breakdown of costs please refer to our relevant pricing document.

Project Approach
1. Technical Review & Survey
Following appointment, Fenster Glazing will review all project documentation and undertake a detailed site survey to verify
dimensions, interfaces and installation requirements. Any discrepancies or technical queries identified will be raised and
resolved prior to manufacture.
2. Procurement & Manufacture
Materials will be procured through our established supply chain following survey completion and approval of all relevant
information. Fabrication drawings will be produced where required to support manufacture and installation activities.
3. Project Management
A dedicated Project Manager and Installation Manager will be assigned to oversee the works from award through to
completion. Pre-start meetings, regular progress updates and proactive communication ensure effective coordination
throughout the project.
4. Installation
Installation works will be undertaken by experienced glazing operatives in accordance with project-specific RAMS, agreed site
phasing and Principal Contractor requirements. Particular consideration is given to working within occupied buildings and live
environments where disruption must be minimised.
5. Quality Assurance & Handover
Quality inspections are undertaken throughout survey, manufacture and installation activities in accordance with our QA
procedures. Upon completion, internal snagging inspections will be carried out prior to handover, with all relevant warranties,
O&M information and project documentation provided as part of the close-out process.
