The board now says which date will be missed, weeks before it is missed

Joseph's dashboard section, built to the brief in JOSEPH-HUB-DEV.md. Seven
tabs: Today (what is going wrong, worst first, with the move that answers it),
The twelve steps (the portfolio as a grid, then each job in full with the
evidence under every tick), Waiting on, Money, What I changed, plus decisions
and messages.

It rests on one number. Every step counts back from the day we go on site, so
the longest lead time still outstanding IS the earliest we could be on site.
On a dated job that says whether the date survives before the week it is
missed in; on an undated one it is the only honest thing to put in the column.
It is arithmetic off the assumed lead times in crm_contract.py and is labelled
an estimate everywhere it appears.

A new route, /api/crm/programme, because the two that existed would both have
lied. crm/contracts is job rows with no steps. crm/delivery is dated tasks,
and every task on these four jobs is undated because none has a site date - so
a board built on it reports that nothing is late about a job buying frames
against a programme nobody has written down. The route also splits managed
from live: 33 contracts read live and 29 are seeded export rows with no PO and
nobody running them, which the page states rather than counting.
