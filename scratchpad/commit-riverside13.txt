Gordon Court found their longest-running exposure was already qualified by page 8
of the proposal they sent. Run here, because I had been quoting the 30-day house
validity for three turns off a GENERATOR FOOTER rather than the document - the
kind of unsourced number I would challenge in anyone else's work.

Verified at source, templates/proposal-content.json, "Quotation Validity":
"All quotations provided by Fenster Glazing & Locks Ltd are valid for 30 days from
the date of issue, unless agreed otherwise. All quotations are subject to final
site survey and measurement verification."

The 30 days is right so every deadline stands, and it now rests on the house
document. The second sentence is the find: Riverside's 1130 x 1530 came from
Adam's enquiry, not a survey, and the pack has no dimensioned opening - so our own
terms already qualify the dimensional risk. It does not rescue the wall-or-roof
question, since a roof vent is not a measurement error.

AND THEIR SHARPER EXCLUSIONS TEST - "we exclude X" is only safe if X is genuinely
somebody else's under the spec - found a second item on my list. Most hold:
builder's work is PHDB's, access is Adam's standing rule, maintenance is the
occupier's RRO duty, Part K balustrading is A Plus's own exclusion. Onward haulage
from our MK13 yard to site does not: Fenster are the installer, so it is ours, and
it was excluded because nobody had priced it. Second of this class in two nights
after the restrictors. Now provisional, covered by brief item 6(b).

The two tests are different: "is a rate or a quantity missing" tells you who to
ask; "is this genuinely somebody else's" tells you whether to be asking at all.

And a trap logged before it happens: their issued proposal excludes site storage

Riverside has no proposal yet and that wording must not go in - A Plus deliver to
Milton Keynes and carry no site address at all.
