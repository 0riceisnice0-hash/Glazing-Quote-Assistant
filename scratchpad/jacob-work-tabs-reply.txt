THE EIGHT, IN THE ORDER THEY APPEAR NOW

1. Today
A digest, not a source. It ranks everything the other seven pages hold into one
"do these today" list, plus six tiles. Delete any page below and this one gets
thinner; it holds no rows of its own.

2. Leads
Every live job Fenster has QUOTED, in one list, ordered by when to go back to
it. Around 210 rows. This is the page Adam asked for on 29/07 and it is the
only fully editable one - set a date, write what was said, change the status or
hand it to somebody, and it survives every rebuild. It merges three sources and
keeps them labelled, because they are not the same class of fact:
- Register (11) - issue date read out of the message that left estimating@
- Mary's records - dated off a tender return date, which is a different clock
- AdminBase (200) - the CRM's word that it is quoted, not the client's

3. Chasing
The verified half of Leads on its own, plus three things Leads does not carry:
Adam's 15-step checklist numbering with the next-chase date, the three priced
jobs that never went out (GBP 73k - Gintare's, not chaseable), and the log of
what this board had wrong. 11 issued, GBP 8.4m out, 4 chaseable today.

4. Ready to send
The 6 drafts I have written for a named human to send from their own mailbox,
with the evidence behind each, what it must not say, plus the ones withdrawn
before anybody sent them and the ones I deliberately did not write.

5. Chase list
The raw AdminBase export - 264 quoted leads across 127 clients, 209 of them
chaseable. It is tier three of Leads shown unmerged, with the caveats attached:
its values are inc VAT and ours are ex, and "Live - Quoted" is a system that
closes nothing rather than 209 live opportunities.

6. Enquiries
Mailbox intake. Who has written to commercial@/jacob@ and what they want -
6 buyers mid-conversation, 22 gone quiet, out of 34 threads. Suppliers and
householders are listed at the bottom so the count adds up. This is the stage
before a quote exists.

7. Out to bid
Public tender notices still open, soonest closing first - 20 live, 8 closing
inside a week. Split into ones Fenster can price itself, main contracts with a
glazing package where the job is finding who is bidding, and word-matches to
read before acting. The email-only leads that are in no public feed are merged
in here and marked.

8. Opportunities
Companies that have just WON a public contract. 1 warm, 6 quoted-before, 112
cold. Nobody is assigned to the cold ones - that needs JAC-2 answered and a
separate sending domain.

ON YOUR QUESTION ABOUT OPPORTUNITIES

Half right, and the half that is wrong matters. They are new names and nobody
has contacted them, so they are not old leads. But they are not leads I sourced
either - they are award notices, which is the weakest signal there is. By the
time an award publishes, the main contractor has already picked their
subcontractors. Seven of the 119 are worth a call today; the other 112 are cold
and blocked.

The pages that actually hold new leads needing contact are Out to bid (a
contract still open, so the enquiry list is being drawn up now) and Enquiries (a
buyer who has written in and has no price yet).

So if you want slot two to be "new leads to contact", renaming Opportunities
will not do it. Merging will: Out to bid plus the 7 warm and known award
winners is one honest page of names nobody has spoken to, and the 112 cold sit
under it collapsed until JAC-2 is answered.
