NaN was my bug, and it was on the Bridport card I added 20 minutes before you saw it.

It stands for "not a number". The card had an empty deadline field, the page tried to count
the days to a date that was not there, and the subtraction produced NaN - so it printed
"NaN days left". Nothing was wrong with the job; it was the board doing arithmetic on a blank.

Your rule is in, and it is in the generator rather than on the card, so the next job that
arrives without a date gets the default automatically instead of publishing NaN again:

- No deadline given -> a default of today + 7 days, written into the state file so it stays
  put. (I did not recompute it on every deploy on purpose - that would give a default that is
  permanently a week away and therefore never arrives.)
- It is labelled everywhere it appears. The chip reads "7 days (DEFAULT, not client-set)" and
  stays amber whatever the count, and opening the job shows the line "DEFAULT - no deadline
  given by the client... Replace it the moment a real date arrives."
- Anyone can overwrite it with a real date later, exactly as you said.

Two jobs had no date and both now show 05/08/2026 as a default:

  1. 10 South Street, Bridport - the shopfront you sent me at 15:44. The client said
     "timescale: not specified".
  2. Redditch Library - no date has ever been set on it. The pack's 26/06 was Gleeds' date to
     the main contractors and has passed, and Leonard White has only ever said "asap". You
     asked him "when are you submitting cost on this one?" in the same email as the GBP 89k
     line, so a real date may come back from him - that is the one to replace first.

The labelling matters more than the date. Five hub dates have previously turned out to be
supplier quote expiries or our own 30-day validity that had quietly become "the client's
deadline", so a default that cannot be told apart from a real return date would be worse than
the blank was. It now says what it is on the face of the card.
