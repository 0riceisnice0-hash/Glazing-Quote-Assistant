Nothing already in mary_checks could see it. Reconciling the total does not -

way: sell minus supply, row by row, against the code table. Nine rows, eight on
their adder to the penny, one at 91.80. Ten selftest variants, including the
dangerous direction - a delivery share folded into the sell must not excuse a
missing uplift, and an unknown code must ASK rather than pass.
