Riverside House: the pack does not corroborate "the openings are newly formed"

Gordon Court sent back three checks off the compliance-route rule. All three paid
out, and one contradicts a ruling this chat broadcast an hour earlier.

Adam's REQ-9 answer said the vents can be any size because the openings are new.
Gordon Court warned that is often true of only part of a building - their own
schedules constrain ground and first floors to existing structural openings while
levels 2-3 are new build. Run on Riverside the answer is not "half" but "not at
all": K1653-04 "EXISTING / PROPOSED ELEVATIONS" is a single set of eight
elevations of a complete, regularly fenestrated building, with no new opening
marked and no AOV drawn anywhere. Adam's word is the only source. So:

  - if the openings are existing, enlarging them is structural work that is in
    nobody's price;
  - the drawings are stamped 24/02303/PAPCR, a PRIOR APPROVAL rather than a full
    permission, and prior-approval conversions normally limit external
    alteration - so "as big as we need" may not be available at all;
  - the second-floor vent sits in the central tower, whose top storey carries two
    ARCHED-HEAD windows on Elevation F against the square-head casement quoted.

Their stale-document check works on a six-drawing pack: the AOV requirement
appears only on K1653-11 and K1653-12, neither ever revised since Mar 24, while
the ground floor plan was revised twice in Nov 25. Probably innocent, one line to
confirm. The pack also holds two layout sets and only the construction-issue one
carries the smoke-vent note.

Refined their ten-second ratio test. Required over gross frame is 57.8%, inside
their 60% flag; A Plus's 1.30 is 75% of gross but exactly 100% of the inner
aperture, 957 x 1357 = 1.2986 m2. The aperture is the real ceiling and therefore
the right denominator. That arithmetic also resolves RFI-2 - the whole frame
opens as one bottom-hung leaf with the transom a glazing bar within the sash,
which is why a single 850mm chain works on 590mm apertures.
