Audited 65 sent pricing documents: they foot, except Gordon Court REV 2

Zac's first ask (dashmsg-97): check the work we have quoted ourselves for
mistakes. New tool, scripts/mary_quote_audit.py - deliberately not the engine's
job. The backtest asks "would Mary have said this number", where a disagreement
usually means the engine is wrong. This asks a question with no opinion in it:
does the document agree with itself and with the supplier quote behind it?

THE ONE ERROR. Target Maintenance - Gordon Court Pricing REV 2.xlsx, 24/07/2026,
the SO_14045 door repair at St John's Terrace Road, Redhill - NOT Chigwell's
Gordon Court, which is a different job with the same name. Row 9, quantity 1:
the Unit Rate cell says 3,381.62 and the Total cell says 3,792.79. At a quantity
of one those must be the same number. They differ by GBP 411.17, and TOTAL*
4,192.79 is built on the higher one. Both cells are hard-typed - the template's
Total = Unit Rate x Qty formula has been overwritten - so the document cannot
self-correct. Whichever figure was intended, the document contradicts itself on
its face, and it is ~10% of a 4,192.79 quote. Not marked DO NOT SEND. FOR ADAM.

EVERYTHING ELSE FOOTS. 65 documents, and FOOT_DOC and FOOT_LINE are otherwise
clean: no other line fails to multiply out, and no document fails to reach its
own TOTAL. That is a genuinely good result and it is worth stating plainly.

IT TOOK SIX FALSE ALARMS TO GET THERE, and each one is now a comment in the
code, because every one of them would have cost Adam a morning:
  - 513 "findings" that were the template's own code adder, on every line in
    the archive. The adder is not written in any column.
  - Crestwood Park 17,779.06 and Wisley 1,419.00 out: addendum rows (TELEFLEX,
    EXTERNAL MASTIC) the reader did not know existed. Both foot to the penny.
  - Feltham 8,920.00 out: same TELEFLEX line, written 'Teleflex ' in mixed case.
  - Coventry Rev 2 39,349.26 out: two addenda rejected for containing a comma
    and a slash, one of them 92 characters long.
  - All three Brandon Estate documents out by exactly their mastic + EDPM,
    because the block is headed OPTIONAL EXTRAS and the test was == "optional".
  - ASHE - CDC 1.00 out, twice: a section heading reading 'Doors' with a stray
    1 in the Qty column, read as a GBP 1.00 addendum.

AND ONE I ALMOST REPORTED. Princess Beatrice House looked short by 7,160.12,
which is 2.500% of the sum of its parts to the penny - so the audit inferred an
unwritten main contractor's discount. It is written down: there is a row saying
'2.5% MCD Discount  -7160.12' that an over-strict label filter had hidden. A gap
that lands on a suspiciously round percentage is far more likely to be a row you
failed to read than a discount somebody failed to write. Look for the row first.
The check stays in, firing on nothing, with that lesson attached.

BUILDUP (182) and SUPPLIER (16) are NOT reported as errors and should not be
read as such - they encode assumptions about how a rate was assembled rather
than pure arithmetic, and separating real findings from template variation there
is the next session's job. Filwood already shows what is in them: seven lines
over 6m2 built on the 0.75 adder rather than 1.25, which is engine evidence, not
a mistake.
