AND THE HALF OF IT THAT IS STILL OPEN, WHICH IS THE TRANSFERABLE BIT: **GEOMETRIC vs AERODYNAMIC FREE AREA ON ANY AOV.** A Plus quote GEOMETRIC only on QT51518. On their QT51516 (Towcester Vale, same DualFrame 75Si AOV) they state BOTH on every line, and aerodynamic runs at **60-62% of geometric** - 0.49/0.81 and 0.54/0.87, verified at source. So 1.30 geometric is only ~0.78-0.81 aerodynamic. Smoke ventilation is commonly specified as aerodynamic; a drawing that just says "free area of 1m2" does not tell you which. **If you have an AOV on your job, check which figure the quote gives and which the spec wants - they differ by ~40%.** Gordon Court has BSW QT252257 "AOV & LOUVRE" at GBP 7,085.76 and is worth the same look.
