The SMA Smart-Wall system you have specified is one we price and buy regularly, so

Four things we would rather raise now than in the week the tender is due:

1. COLOUR. The schedule asks for brown to match the existing frames, but gives no
   RAL or BS number, and a powder coat batch cannot be ordered against "similar to
   existing". We can take a reading on site if that is easiest. Related - the
   photograph enclosed as the example of the new door is an anthracite grey door,
   which reads against the brown specified in the text. Could you confirm which is
   intended?

2. THE INTERCOMS. The schedule asks for the existing intercoms to be disconnected
   from the old doorsets, reconnected to the new, and proven working on completion.
   The hardware listed for the new doors is a mechanical cylinder with a thumbturn
   inside, and nothing else - no electric release, keep, power transfer or door
   loop. As specified the panel would still call the flat, but nothing would release
   the door. So either an electric release is intended and needs adding to the
   specification, or the panels are to be call-and-speak only. We need to know
   which, because clause 2.1.4 asks us to name our subcontractors with the tender
   and this element is a door-entry specialist's.

3. THE BLOCK NUMBERING. The particulars give the blocks as 1-6, 7-16, 17-23, 24-31
   and 32-39. The nameplates in your own photographs read 1-6, 7-15, 16-23, 24-31
   and 32-39 - flat 16 sits on the third entrance, not the second - and the estate
   plan agrees. Your key count settles it: at 3 per flat plus 3 per doorset, 38
   dwellings gives exactly the 129 keys you have asked for, where 39 would give 132.
   It matters to us because the keys are five differs, and against the ranges as
   written we would cut the wrong quantity per cylinder. Could you confirm the
   entrance-by-entrance split so we key them correctly?

4. THERMAL PERFORMANCE, and this one is worth settling before the Section 20
   consultation rather than after. The particulars set no U-value. SMA's own
   published figure for a Smart-Wall door is 1.8 W/m2K, which is above the limiting
   standard Approved Document L applies to a replacement door in an existing
   building. We are content to tender the system you have specified, but if Building
   Control take a view on it later it becomes a variation on a price your
   leaseholders will already have been consulted on.

We are already working with Anton Antonov on Earleswood Court, so you should find us
on your system.
