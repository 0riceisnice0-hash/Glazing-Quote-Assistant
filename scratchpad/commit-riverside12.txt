29 days to ask A Plus cheaply, and an exclusion that deserved a number

Gordon Court took last night's validity arithmetic and found the sharper
consequence: a lapsing supplier quote is a deadline for every question you still
want to ask that supplier. Anything sent while the quote is live is priced
against it - same job, same rates, they add lines. Anything after is a fresh
enquiry.

  A Plus QT51518, dated 27/07, 30 days -> lapses 26/08 -> 29 days from today

It bites hardest on the item asking for a RESIZED unit: asked now that is a
revision I can set against GBP 4,845.22 and read the delta; asked in September it
is a new number with no anchor. So two dates now, answering different questions -
27/07 (past) was the last date we could ISSUE and stay covered; 26/08 is the last
date we can ASK cheaply.

AND THEIR RATE-VERSUS-QUANTITY LENS FOUND SOMETHING I HAD GOT WRONG. Verified
CW_SUPPLY_M2 850 / CW_LABOUR_M2 150 in mary_pricing - they were right that
curtain walling has a rate and lacks a quantity, which is a different problem with
a different owner. Sorting my own items the same way put the window restrictors in
"quantity known, no rate anywhere" - a supplier question - when I had them in the
exclusions list.

They should not have been there. A Plus's own notes say the actuators "will not
act as window restrictors", that "the facade contractor / fabricator" should fit
them 50mm beyond the stroke, and that A Plus will not be liable for damage to the
vent if none is fitted. Fenster are the installer, so that is us, on a life-safety
system. Excluding them may still be right but it is a call to make against a
number. Now Part One item 8 and reclassified excluded -> provisional.

The general form, in AI.md: run the rate/quantity sort across an EXCLUSIONS list,
not just an unpriced one. Sometimes an exclusion is an unanswered supplier
question wearing an exclusion's clothes.

Price unchanged at GBP 5,990.22, still not issued. Checks 0 failed, 3 questions
across 21 items. Selftest passes.
