Update the Work dashboard and operating rules as follows.

## Purpose of Jacob

Jacob has two core responsibilities:

1. Find new commercial glazing opportunities for Fenster by searching relevant public sources.
2. Keep Fenster on top of leads that have already been quoted and require follow-up.

Estimating is handled separately. Jacob must not manage the estimating process.

## Work Dashboard Structure

The Work section must appear in this order:

1. Today
2. Opportunities
3. Leads
4. Ready to Send

### 1. Today

Today must act as the main daily action list.

It must clearly show every action that needs attention today, including:

* Leads due to be chased today.
* Overdue lead chases.
* New opportunities requiring review.
* Drafts ready for review or sending.
* Records missing a next action, owner or deadline.

Every item shown on Today must include:

* Client.
* Project.
* Current stage.
* Owner.
* Next action.
* Action deadline.
* Reason it appears on Today.

The next action must be clearly visible and written as a specific instruction, for example:

* Call client for tender outcome.
* Send first follow-up email.
* Confirm whether the project was awarded.
* Review newly found tender.
* Identify bidding main contractors.
* Close lead due to no response.

Today must pull these actions from the relevant individual pages. It must not hold separate duplicate records.

### 2. Opportunities

Opportunities must contain new commercial glazing work that Jacob has found and that Fenster has not yet contacted.

This may include:

* Open public tenders.
* Commercial window, door, curtain walling or glazing packages.
* Main contracts where Jacob needs to identify the bidding contractors.
* Relevant contract awards where there is still a credible contact opportunity.
* Other suitable commercial opportunities found online.

Each opportunity must show:

* Company or authority.
* Project.
* Location.
* Scope.
* Source.
* Deadline, where applicable.
* Why it is relevant to Fenster.
* Recommended next action.
* Owner or unassigned status.
* Current status.

Cold or weak award notices must not be presented as equal to genuine open tender opportunities. They may remain in a lower-priority or collapsed prospecting section.

### 3. Leads

Leads must contain qualified work that Fenster has decided to pursue or has already quoted.

Incoming enquiries are qualified by a human before reaching Jacob. Estimating is handled by a separate estimating bot.

Jacob must therefore focus on:

* Quotations that have been issued.
* Leads awaiting follow-up.
* Leads awaiting a client decision.
* Leads requiring revised contact.
* Leads that should be closed, marked won or marked lost.
* Any priced jobs that were prepared but not issued, where these have been passed to Jacob.

Each lead must clearly show:

* Client.
* Project.
* Quote value excluding VAT.
* Quote issue date.
* Current stage.
* Owner.
* Last meaningful contact.
* Next action.
* Next-action deadline.
* Chase history.
* Expected decision date, where known.
* Status such as live, won, lost, on hold or closed.

The next action must be visible directly on the Leads page and must also feed through to Today when due.

The separate Chasing and Chase List pages are no longer working pages.

Any useful information from Chasing, including checklist stages, next-chase dates and chase history, must be moved into Leads.

The raw AdminBase Chase List may remain under System or Data for audit purposes, but it must only act as a source feeding Leads.

### 4. Ready to Send

Ready to Send must contain drafts Jacob has prepared for a named member of Fenster staff to review and send.

Each draft must show:

* The linked client and project.
* Intended recipient.
* Intended sender.
* Purpose of the message.
* Evidence used.
* Any wording that must be avoided.
* Approval status.

Jacob must not send these messages automatically unless separately and explicitly authorised.
