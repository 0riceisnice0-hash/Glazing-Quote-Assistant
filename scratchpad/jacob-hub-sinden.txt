**A fix underneath all this that matters more than the client.** Writing a truer state onto a
researched row - "re-enquired, our price is the late one" - made it **vanish** from the chase list
and off the daily email, because that list is filtered on three literal state strings. Researching
the most urgent row on the board is not a reason to hide it. A worked row now keeps its place
whatever state it is given, and a blocked one is labelled rather than dropped.
