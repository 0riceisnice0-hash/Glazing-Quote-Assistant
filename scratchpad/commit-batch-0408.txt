A queue of 78 read as one picture: three new jobs, and a supplier who left with our rate

The front desk had backed up to 78 work orders spanning eleven days. Most of it
was one of five conversations arriving a message at a time, so the useful move
was to read the whole thing before touching any of it.

Thirty-seven went to the chats that own them - fifteen on Brocks Hill, where
Adam's question about the seven excluded doors got "include for the additional
doors" back from Martin Moore; five on Filwood, which turns out to have gone out
at 12:28 on the 30th with Stepnell acknowledging seven minutes later. Thirty-seven
more were settled history or noise. Forty-one remain and come round next pass.

Three were genuinely new and now have chats. Totteridge Academy wants a cost
review by Friday, and the trap there is that we quoted the same scheme for Conamar
as well, so searching Borras alone finds half of it. Addison Avenue is not really
a pricing job - Harris Calnan already have client approval for a competitor's
product, and the detail drawing, the installed photographs and the thermal figures
are what decide whether our price is read at all. Alice Billings runs through the
eque2 portal, the fourth portal client this month, and Gintare has already asked
the question that decides the quantities and not been answered.

Two supplier changes matter more than any of it. Martin Gregory has resigned from
CN Glass - and he is the man who agreed the sixty pounds a square metre that Stoke
Park's glass order was placed against on the 27th, on an email rather than a
quotation. A verbal rate is only as durable as the person who gave it. And AGF
have put a date on Reynaers: anything ordered on or after 27 August is repriced,
which lands directly on the Totteridge review.

The rate work turned up a defect worth more than the three quotes it came from.
Vetroseal bill a delivery charge as a glass line carrying a fake 0.300 square
metres. Divide goods by total area, as any miner would, and the rate reads 4.4%
high with nothing visibly wrong. Charge lines have to come out before mining.

No email to Adam. Nothing here is an error or a moved number, the two things that
earn the interruption; the deadlines belong to the chats that now hold them.
