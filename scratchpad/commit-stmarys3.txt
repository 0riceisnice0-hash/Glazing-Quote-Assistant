The door schedule nobody had read - and the average that had to be computed

Third turn on St Mary's. No work order and both handoffs closed, so I went at the open
items. Opened door schedule 2376-08 for the first time; the job had been priced entirely
off the window schedule.

IT CORRECTS OUR OWN ANALYSIS. The 1.2 W/m2K for external doors is expressly "an area
weighted average u-value - not a centre pane value". Our manifest recorded the basis as
"per element" throughout, and that was wrong for the doors. SM5 Wexham's rule says you may
not reject an element against an average - I had nearly walked into that trap a second time.

THE FINDING SURVIVES, BECAUSE THE AVERAGE WAS COMPUTED RATHER THAN ASSERTED. On 22.078 m2
of Smart Wall Pocket at SMA's published 1.8, plus at most 20.367 m2 of MC600, the MC600
would have to achieve 0.55 W/m2K for the package to average 1.2. A generous 1.0 gives 1.42;
1.4 gives 1.61. The doors miss on the architect's own averaging basis, which is a far
stronger thing to put to a client than a claim about one element.

IT ALSO STRENGTHENS THE CASE. 2376-08 carries per-door notes on D.01, D.17, D.22 and D.26
reading "Door to achieve min u-value of 1.4W/m2k". So 1.4 is stated in the window schedule,
the door schedule and our own proposal - it no longer depends on the energy annex at all,
and ET&S cannot dispose of it by ruling EDG02 inapplicable.

NEW REQ-19: four things in that schedule are in nobody's price. Fobbed readers explicitly
required on D.01 and D.14, against our exclusion of access control and our own clarification
that fobbed reader compatibility "requires further review" - never done, and Bellview's seven
units carry no strike, rectifier or transfer hinge. Anti-ligature pull handles, BS 7352 hinges
and kicking plates specified and not quoted, which on a special needs school is safeguarding
rather than finish. "No locking mechanism or latch" on four doors against the concealed panic
bars we priced on all seven - and Aplus flagged that exact ambiguity in writing on their
alternative while Bellview defaulted silently. And the two architect's schedules disagree on
external door sizes, with only D.01 mapping cleanly - which is also the bifold our proposal
substituted for French doors without written acceptance.

TWO FALSE LEADS CLEARED SO NOBODY REPEATS THEM. Triage's John North Hall note warns that
replacement jobs put strip-out, making good and disposal in scope. Only the strip-out lands
on us here - making good and decoration are the client's own SOW section 8, so our Internal
Finishing exclusion is safe and RFI-4 should not be widened. And this pack carries no tender
validity clause at all, so the 90-day Section 20 trap does not apply. Also negative: the SMA
commercial brochure is a 2009 corporate document with no U-values, so the "Smart Wall vs
Smart Wall POCKET" and MC600 caveats stay open - only SMA can close them.
