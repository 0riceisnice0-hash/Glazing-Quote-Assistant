Fixed a bug in the stale-draft sweep that was hiding our own letter

Gordon Court built scripts/mary_stale_drafts.py and asked every chat to run it.
Run here, our A Plus letter was absent from the report entirely - not under due,
not under undated - while the sweep concluded "Nothing expired". Their note said
the tool sees it at 26/08. It parses it; it never printed it.

The bug: days<0 to expired, days<=warn_days to due, and no else. Any dated draft
more than a fortnight out was parsed, dated and silently dropped. Proved before
changing anything - the same file shows correctly at 6 days on --today 2026-08-20
and expired on 2026-08-27, so parsing was always right and only reporting was
blind.

Fixed with a DATED, NOT YET DUE bucket, reasoning in the docstring. All three date
views verified, exit-1-on-expiry still fires, and scan() had no callers outside
prose so extending the return signature was safe.

Why it was not cosmetic: a sweep that shows a dated draft only in the last
fortnight of its life shows it exactly when acting has stopped being comfortable.
The letter's whole argument is "send this while the quote is live" and 15 of its
29 days would have passed unmentioned.
