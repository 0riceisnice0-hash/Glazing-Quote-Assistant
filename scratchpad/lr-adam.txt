3. WHAT THE ER ADDS TO OUR COST

None of this was visible before today.

- Secured by Design across all windows, external doors and ironmongery.
- PAS 24:2007 on all doors, and external and front doors must be bought as DOOR SETS.
- Laminated safety glass to BS EN 356 class P2A - and the drawings apply it to ALL GROUND

  as a cost.
