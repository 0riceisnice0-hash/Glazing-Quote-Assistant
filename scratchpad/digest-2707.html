3. RIVERSIDE AOV - NO, IT DOES NOT MAKE 1.5m2

Aplus QT51518, dated today. 2no DualFrame 75Si, 1130 x 1530, white, 155mm subcill, open out, 850mm stroke single chain.

Geometric free area 1.30 m2, based on a 50mm reveal. You need 1.5 m2, so it is 0.20 m2 short.

Aplus have given the fix on page 2. At 1235 x 1583 the same configuration achieves 1.5 m2, using 900mm chains instead of 850mm.

Price as quoted is GBP 4,845.22 net ex VAT for the pair - frames 4,662.15, glass 171.31, energy surcharge 11.76. Supply only, delivered, no installation. Valid 30 days.

Three things in their AOV notes worth knowing before ordering.

Cables are not run through the mullions. They fix the actuator and leave about 2m of flex coiled at the vent. Anything beyond that is someone else's scope.

The actuators are not restrictors. Aplus disclaim liability for damage if a separate restrictor is not fitted, set 50mm beyond the stroke.

If the vents sit below 2.5m from finished floor they flag a trap hazard under BS EN 60335-2, and below 1100mm they need anti-fall protection under Part K, which they exclude.

The quote is specified with no PAS24, no restrictor, no handle and no casement locking. Normal for a smoke vent, but worth confirming against what Riverside expects.

5. VESUVIUS WAY - THE DOORS MUST BE 60 MINUTE FIRE RATED AND MY BUDGET DID NOT PRICE IT - DEADLINE THURSDAY

Gintare asked Steve about this at 09:35 today. She is right, and it is bigger than a door question.

I have checked it at source. JHA NBS Section 2, section L20, dated 15/07/2026:

External Doors - "60 Min Insulated steel-core external open-out single-leaf door. PPC Galvanised double skinned door leaf with PPC Galvanised 90mm enclosed frame." Vision panel 572 x 572, U-value 1.2.

External Doors Curtain Walling - "60 Min Door installed in curtain wall to manufacturers design."

External Doors Louvered - 60 Min, steel-core, louvred leaf, louvres to comply with the NOVA acoustic report.

Fire and smoke resistance - tested to BS EN 1634-1 or 1634-3, third-party certified, fire door schedule to Building Control before order.

What that does to my GBP 110,551.98.

The 2no doors at 1000 x 2450 are in at GBP 4,683.56, priced as standard Senior SPD150 aluminium off BSW glazed-door medians. The spec wants insulated steel-core, which is a different product family - closer to Strongdor or Aluminium Fire Systems than to any aluminium fabricator.

Worse, the two curtain wall screens carry doors within them. The welfare screen at GBP 8,377.50 and the office entrance screen at GBP 41,000.00. That is GBP 49,377.50, some 45% of the budget, sitting on SF52 screens whose doors the spec says must be 60 minute rated. A 60 minute door does not go into a standard SF52 screen - those bays need a tested fire-rated screen system.

So the budget is understated, and I cannot tell you by how much until a fire-screen specialist prices it. This compounds the Senior problem I raised on Monday: none of BSW, Aplus or Bellview fabricate Senior, and now the door package needs a fire specialist on top of that.

Aluminium Fire Systems are already quoting us on Manor Lodge - Julian Ward, Q7666. They are the obvious people to ask, today, if Thursday is still the target.
