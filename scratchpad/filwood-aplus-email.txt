Adam,

Aplus have quoted Filwood too - QT51510 came in this morning. Deadline is Thursday.

The number looks like good news and is not. But it has settled three arguments, and it has raised one
that matters more than the price.


THE HEADLINE

Aplus GBP 34,445.91. BSW GBP 46,067.59. That is GBP 11,621.68 apart - and they are not the same scope.

Aplus say, in three words on page 16: "Panels by others."

They have left 46.09 m2 of spandrel, base and ventilation-zone infill out - 37.5% of the elevation.
It is visible line by line: every non-glazed aperture is listed as a 32mm opening with no product
against it, and three of ED-06's seven segments have no glass line at all. BSW include all of it, as
70 flat aluminium panels.

Break-even is GBP 252.15 per m2 of panel. Below that Aplus is genuinely cheaper, above it BSW is.

I cannot tell you which. BSW bundle the panels into the element price with no extractable figure,
Aplus exclude them, and our rate register has no panel or spandrel category at all. It needs a price,
not an estimate. Please do not let GBP 34,445.91 be read as a saving.


THE ONE THAT MATTERS MORE

Page 16, again their words: "Quoted in STII, these will only reach 1.8/1.9 U Value."

The drawing schedule asks for 1.0 per element.

BSW told us the same thing on 24 July in different words - performance met "for glazing only... they are
non rebated". Aplus have now put a number on it. Their standard terms go further still: "Commercial
doors and framing will be supplied with a U-Value of up to 3.0".

Two independent fabricators, two systems, both saying no. At that point it stops being a supplier
problem. A standard commercial shopfront system is not thermally broken to curtain-walling standard and
does not get to 1.0 - which is very probably why the architect specified Aluprof and issued the drawings
to Aluprof directly in October.

We have not approached a single Aluprof fabricator. I think the Aluprof specification is not a
formality to engineer around; it may be the only route to the performance, and if it is, both our
prices are for the wrong product.

Aplus also state "STII doors have no formal acoustic test data" - ED-06 needs Rw 32 dB - and "Glass
quoted has a g value of 0.66" against a required 0.5 to 0.6. That last one is useful: it is the first
hard number anyone has given us on the g-value, and it confirms neither quote has solar control glass
in it.


THREE ARGUMENTS SETTLED

1. The sizes. Aplus set out to the trade bill exactly - 4930, 5550, and 6315 x 3105 - and their ED-06
   segments run 300 / 1200 / 700 / 600, which is the dimension string printed on the drawing. So BSW's
   4850 / 4800 / 6250 are wrong, and our own 3150 for ED-06 is a typo for 3105.

2. The g-value. 0.66 quoted against 0.5-0.6 required.

3. Delivery. Aplus: "All orders are priced as Ex-Works", free only over GBP 5,000 AND within 50 miles of
   Watford. Bristol is about 105 miles. BSW are ex works too and their quote carries no terms page at
   all. Carriage is in neither number.
