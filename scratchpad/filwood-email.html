WHAT IS RIGHT

BSW quote 0000000507 (24/07) reconciles to the penny. All seven positions carried, nothing dropped,

THE FIVE THAT MATTER
1. INSTALLATION IS GBP 14,946 SHORT.

The template INSTALLATION formula read the product code DAD and gave us GBP 500 x 7 elements. That code
is for a double aluminium door on its own, not a 17.6 m2 screen. At our own CW labour rate of GBP 150/m2
the number is GBP 18,446.32.

Worse, the proposal excludes scaffold, MEWPs and towers while including installation of elements
3,570 mm tall. So there is no access plant in the price either.

Correct the install line and the tender becomes GBP 82,013.90 - within GBP 2,797 of the GBP 84,810.59
I built independently on 17 July with no supplier quote at all. Two different routes landing in the same
place is the best evidence I can give you that GBP 3,500 is the error.

2. THE LPS 1175 SR2 DOORSET ON ED-06 IS NOT PRICED.

The drawing schedule requires LPS 1175 SR2 security on ED-06. BSW have quoted a standard commercial
doorset - electric strike, electric latch, rectifier, closer. There is no reference to LPS 1175, SR2 or
any LPCB certification anywhere on the quote. That is a different, tested product.

3. THE SOLAR CONTROL GLASS IS NOT PRICED ON ANY ELEMENT.

All three types require a g-value of 0.5-0.6. BSW quoted plain 6.8 laminated / 4 toughened and
8.8 laminated / 6 toughened. No coating is named and no g-value is stated. Clear glass sits around 0.7.

The cross-check: BSW works out at GBP 374.61/m2. My 17 July build was GBP 359.60/m2 median plus a
GBP 45/m2 spec uplift. The gap is roughly the coating that is missing.

4. THE BILL SPECIFIES ALUPROF. WE ARE OFFERING SMA SHOPLINE WITH NOTHING SAID ABOUT IT.

The trade bill names Aluprof with their address and website. The materials schedule gives Manufacturer =
Aluprof. Drawing 31551's own revision history reads "Issued to Aluprof 22.10.2025" - the architect has
been working with them directly.

The ITT says value engineering is welcome but "must be fully detailed indicating any areas of
non-compliance and be accompanied by a compliant bid". We have neither. This is the third time this
month - Technal/Modeal on Princess Beatrice, Senior on Vesuvius, Aluprof here.

5. THE VENTILATION ZONE IS PRICED AS SOLID ALUMINIUM SHEET.

The top 660 mm of every screen is labelled Ventilation Zone on the drawing and tagged to Cadisch
expanded mesh, with four "mullion behind mesh" notes. BSW's field counts do reconcile with the drawing,
but every non-glazed field is priced as a flat aluminium panel. Either the mesh and the louvre are ours
and unpriced, or they are excluded and the retail units have no ventilation.
