Aplus needs 9 panes at 32mm (8.33 m2) - the door head and transom panes. Vetroseal quoted every single line as 8.8L-16-4T, which is 28.8mm. The 32mm units need their own make-up and their own rate.

3. MONEY

Vetroseal's own rate on this quote is GBP 110.00/m2 goods, plus the energy surcharge at GBP 0.13/kg (GBP 4.15/m2 here) - GBP 114.15/m2 all in.

At that rate the full final list is about GBP 14,930 net against the GBP 12,012.88 quoted - roughly GBP 2,920 more, before whatever the 32mm make-up adds.

CN Glass quoted the same make-up at GBP 60/m2 inc energy on 01/07 (Steve's thread with Martin Gregory), plus GBP 10/m2 if the inner leaf goes to 6mm toughened softcoat. The full final list at that rate is about GBP 7,850 - some GBP 7,000 below Vetroseal.

Our price to Borras is GBP 104,660.17 ex VAT (pricing doc rev 15/07) with the glass inside the unit rates, so any increase comes straight off the margin.
