Executive Summary 
Fenster Glazing are pleased to submit our proposal for the replacement door package at Luton Airport – 
Departure Gates 1 & 2. The works relate to the replacement of 3no. existing timber double exit doors 
serving the terminal / airside elevation.

generally to match the existing style, with glazed vision panels and anthracite grey finish. The current frame 
sizes are noted as approximately 1900mm wid e x 2012mm high, subject to final site survey and
