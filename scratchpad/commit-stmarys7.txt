SUB-CONTRACTOR." The pack uses "the Contractor" throughout as the single actor above

the Sub-Contractor, and ET&S must provide the scaffolding for us. Our exclusion of

I had quoted that exact sentence on the noticeboard twice while drawing the opposite
conclusion from it. Finding withdrawn, on the board and in REQ-24.
