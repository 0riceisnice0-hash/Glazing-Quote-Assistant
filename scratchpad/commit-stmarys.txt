St Mary's: the arithmetic was flawless and the energy annex was never opened

First substantive turn of the st-marys job chat. The GBP 174,546.37 quote went out on
17/07; this turn audited what it actually covers. The price stands.

The build is clean, and that is worth recording as loudly as the problems: all 31
Sheerline types reconcile to BSW QT252799 exactly on quantity AND line total, all 7 SMA
lines to Bellview 0000000483 at the 15% discounted figure to the penny, every unit rate
follows the house formula, and the global install line of GBP 21,915.05 reconciles to
the penny against the labour codes. The Filwood trap did not bite on the biggest line -
Type AK is correctly coded CW with GBP 3,055.05 of curtain-wall labour at GBP 150/m2.

What was wrong was quieter than the sums.

THE PACK SETS TWO U-VALUES AND WE QUOTED THE LOOSER ONE. Window schedule 2376-09 says
1.4 W/m2K, 33 times, and the proposal promises 1.4. EDG02 "Energy and Carbon Design
Guidelines - Building Fabric", in the same 08/07 pack, sets the refurbishment minimum at
1.3 windows, 1.2 external doors and a g-value of 0.4-0.3. The energy annex is filed under
sustainability, nobody opens it, and it is the tighter document. Neither supplier states
a U-value at all - BSW give only a centre-pane Ug, and Bellview state no U-value and no
coating of any kind across GBP 30,352.38 of doors and MC600 curtain walling. No solar
control appears in either quote, so the g-value is definitely not priced. REQ-15.

THE SM5 WEXHAM ERROR, LIVE ON A SENT QUOTE. Type G needs a top-hung vent; Bellview quoted
a door and two fixed fields with "1 x prepared for a thickness of 28mm", and BSW fill that
aperture with an 854x900 Sheerline casement - a 70mm window inside a 100mm Smart Wall
frame, in a 28mm pocket. BSW themselves ruled that coupling impossible on 24/07.
GBP 8,499.66 of sell. REQ-16.

THREE UNDEFINED BOUNDARIES (REQ-17). We exclude scaffold and MEWPs while the preliminaries
require the Contractor to provide all scaffolding "for himself and any Sub-Contractor",
on 55.97 m2 of glazing 3.62 m or taller. SOW item 1.09 allocates strip-out and disposal of
the existing windows into item 6.01 - our line - and we never named it. Manifestation is
in neither quote and neither list.

AND THE RECORD WAS WRONG ABOUT THE SUPPLIER BACKING. It is BSW + Bellview, not BSW + Aplus.
Aplus QP70172 is dated five days after we submitted, is Technal not Sheerline/SMA, and is
quoted unglazed - reordering against it would have bought a different job with no glass.

Commercial: both supplier quotes lapse mid-August but site start is 14/09 with GBP 500/day
delay damages, so GBP 91,409.18 of cost is unfixed before day one.

One false alarm caught before it was raised: BSW write obscure glazing as "ObsTuff", so a
search for "obscure" returns zero on a quote that carries 9 obscure panes.

Also found five handoffs still addressed to job keys that do not exist - riverside,
chester-thomas, manor-house, ninn-lane, lower-range - including a 07/08 deadline and
GBP 100,730 of quoted work. Handed back to triage.

Checks manifest fires 3 FAIL + 1 ASK, all of them the findings above.
