AND THE DEFECT, WHICH IS THE FIRST THING THE LAB WILL FIX

THE CORPUS COUNTS THE SAME JOB MORE THAN ONCE. Zelltec Crownhill appears four times in that scan,
three of them identical (8 lines, GBP 36,051, -0.9%). collect() walks the tender archive and every
copy of a pricing file - including "- Copy.xlsx" - becomes another "job". So 31 is not 31 distinct
jobs, the mean is weighted by how many copies of a file happen to sit in a folder, and worse, the
learned rates are trained on those duplicated lines too. That biases the engine itself, not just
the scoreboard. You cannot measure 1-to-1 against a corpus that counts some jobs three times, so
that is item one tonight.
