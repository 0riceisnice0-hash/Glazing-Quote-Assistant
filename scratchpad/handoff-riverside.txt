THE FREE AREA - AND THE QUESTION NOBODY HAS ASKED

Aplus state "Geometric free area = 1.30m2, based on a 50mm reveal", and on page 2: "Maximum free area possible with the current sizes and AOV size limitations. Increasing the height to allow us to use 900mm chains instead of 850mm will increase the free area, as well as widening the frame (1235 x 1583 would achieve a 1.5m2 in this configuration)." Because they size a SINGLE frame to reach 1.5m2, Aplus are reading Adam's requirement as per vent, and their 1.30m2 as per vent. That is the basis of the answer already given to Adam and of REQ-9 - 1.30 against 1.5, 0.20m2 short.

But QT51518 quotes GEOMETRIC free area only. Aplus's other current quote for the same product family - QT51516, Towcester Vale, 24/07, same DualFrame 75Si AOV - states BOTH figures on every line: 810x1335 gives geometric 0.81m2 / aerodynamic 0.49m2, and 1205x1335 gives geometric 0.87m2 / aerodynamic 0.54m2. Aerodynamic comes out at 60-62% of geometric on both.

Smoke ventilation requirements are commonly specified as AERODYNAMIC free area. If Riverside's 1.5m2 is aerodynamic, then 1.30m2 geometric is roughly 0.8m2 aerodynamic - about half the requirement, not 0.20m2 short. And Aplus's proposed fix of 1235x1583, which reaches 1.5m2 GEOMETRIC, would be about 0.9m2 aerodynamic and still well short. Treat those percentages as indicative only: they come from different sizes and a different chain stroke (Towcester is 900mm, Riverside 850mm). Aplus must state the aerodynamic figure for the actual Riverside sizes.

So there are two unresolved variables, not one: per vent or total, and geometric or aerodynamic. I have updated REQ-9 on the hub to ask both.
