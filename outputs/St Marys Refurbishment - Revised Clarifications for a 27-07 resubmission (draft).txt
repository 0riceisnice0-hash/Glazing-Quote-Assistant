1. THERMAL PERFORMANCE - EXTERNAL DOOR AND SCREEN ELEMENTS   [NEW - IMPORTANT]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The external door and screen elements are offered in SMA Smart Wall Pocket, for which the
manufacturer publishes a thermal performance of 1.8 W/m2K for doors and 1.4 W/m2K for
screens. Our price is based on that system as quoted.

We have therefore NOT allowed for the external door elements to achieve either the
1.4 W/m2K stated on window schedule 2376-09 and door schedule 2376-08, or the 1.2 W/m2K
area-weighted average for External Doors stated in EDG02 Energy and Carbon Design
Guidelines - Building Fabric. Should either figure be required, a thermally broken door
system will be necessary; this is a change of system rather than a change of glazing and
would be subject to re-design, re-quotation and a revised programme.

The SMA MC600 Plus curtain walling offered to Type AK is a separate, thermally broken
system and is not affected by the above; its performance is to be confirmed by the
manufacturer's calculation.

We would welcome the opportunity to price a compliant alternative if required.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THERMAL PERFORMANCE - WINDOWS, AND SOLAR CONTROL   [NEW]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The windows are offered with a 6.8 laminated / 18mm cavity / 4mm toughened low-emissivity
unit with warm-edge spacer, on the basis of achieving the 1.4 W/m2K stated on window
schedule 2376-09. A whole-window U-value calculation can be provided on request.

We have NOT allowed for the 1.3 W/m2K refurbishment figure for windows, nor for the
glazing g-value of 0.4-0.3, both stated in EDG02. The g-value in particular requires a
solar control coating which is not included. Please confirm which document governs the
package - window schedule 2376-09 or EDG02 - and we will price any difference.
