1. THE SMOKE VENTILATION - THREE QUESTIONS, ONE PACKAGE   [ARKON, sheets 52003 and 14001-14005]
=====================================================================================
Window schedule 5244-ARK-52003 carries a block headed "AOV SMOKE SHAFT LOUVRE": 3no WN_7
marked "AOV" in Corridors 1-1/1-2/1-3, and 4no WL_1 noted "Louvres to smoke shaft" at levels
0 to 3. We have priced all seven and would like the design intent confirmed before order.

1.1  DO THE FOUR SMOKE SHAFT LOUVRES SURVIVE THE OCTOBER 2025 CHANGE?
     Fire strategy drawings 14003 and 14004 at rev 02, and 14005 at rev 01, all dated
     09.10.2025, record "Updated to suit fire officers comments... Smoke shaft omitted" and
     "Smoke shafts omitted. Mechanical extract vent added." Those sheets have since been
     revised to rev 04-06 (17.02.2026) without the omission being reversed. The window
     schedules are dated 08.09.2025 and carry no revision.
     If the shafts are deleted, are the 4no WL_1 louvres still required? We would rather
     raise a credit now than supply units that are no longer in the design.

1.2  WHAT DUTY DO THE THREE CORRIDOR AOVs SERVE, AND ARE THEY WALL OR ROOF UNITS?
     The fire strategy legend states two different figures - "AOV. 1.5m2 clear opening area"
     and "SV. NSHEV. 0.4m2 clear opening area minimum". The NBS specifies the 1.5m2 unit as
     a ROOF-mounted AXS140 lobby ventilator on a horizontal kerb, and separately a
     WALL-mounted Coltite glazed lobby ventilator. WN_7 is scheduled as a wall unit at
     910 x 2100.
     Please confirm which duty applies to WN_7 and whether the position is a wall or a roof
     vent. It determines the product, and we do not want to supply the wrong one.
     We are asking our supplier in parallel for the geometric free area their quoted units
     achieve, and for the maximum achievable within the existing 910 x 2100 opening, since
     the installation note fixes ground and first floor to the existing openings. That is
     our half of the question and we are not waiting on you for it.

1.3  IS THE COLT PACKAGE IN OUR SCOPE?
     NBS L20 clause 630 specifies a full Colt system - AXS140 roof ventilators, Defender
     smoke dampers, decorative louvre grilles, Vent Control Panels and the OPV control
     equipment. We have assumed the control system, dampers and roof units are a specialist
     smoke-ventilation contractor's and have priced only the units on the window schedule.
     Please confirm that assumption is right.

6. WHICH WINDOWS ARE OBSCURE-GLAZED?   [ARKON, sheets 21005-21008 and 52002/52003]
=====================================================================================
The proposed elevations carry a materials legend including "FR - Frosted Glass", and FR
is marked against 9 individual windows - 7 on the East elevation (21005 rev 03) and 2 on
the West (21006 rev 03). The North elevation (21008 rev 04) carries the legend but marks
no FR at all, and the South elevation (21007 rev 03) carries no materials legend.

We cannot reconcile that against the window schedules, and we would like to flag why
rather than guess: the three elevations that mark frosted glass carry no window
references, and the one elevation that carries window references carries no materials
legend. There is no sheet in the pack on which a window type and its glazing treatment
appear together.

Our supplier has priced obscure glazing on 23 units - WN_1 11no, WE_3 10no and WE_14 2no
- which is more than the elevations mark. We are content with that as a commercial
position and are not asking for a credit. But somebody has decided which windows are
obscure and it was not the elevations, so please confirm the intended extent, and add an
obscure-glazing column to the window schedules if one is intended. Bathroom and en-suite
privacy is not something we want to resolve by inference.

Related and worth one line: the ObsTuff make-up quoted to us carries no solar coating,
while the schedules require a g-value of 0.36 or better. We have asked our supplier to
state the figure and to price a compliant obscure unit if it falls short. That one is
ours to resolve and we are not asking you to do anything about it.
