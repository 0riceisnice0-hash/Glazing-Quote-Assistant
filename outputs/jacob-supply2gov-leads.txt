THE WARNING, because it is the reason this is only two and not twenty-seven.

Today's alert claims 27 opportunities matching our keywords. The attached HTML lists 10,
of which 8 are unique - it duplicates entries. Across all four alerts we have had
(24, 27, 28, 29 July) there are 30 unique items and FIFTEEN OF THEM ARE
ContractAwardNotice or PriorInformationNotice. Those are records of contracts already
awarded to somebody else. They are not opportunities and no amount of subscription
upgrade turns them into one.

I had this wrong myself until I parsed the attachments this morning. On 28/07 I recorded
that glazing work was stacking up behind the Upgrade button and named four items. Two of
them - "Renewal and Upgrade of Entrance Doors and Door Entry Systems at The Grove,
Swanscombe" and "Supply & Install of Windows & Doors" - are ContractAwardNotices. They
were never available to us.

So if the subscription upgrade ever comes up as a commercial question, the honest number
is that four days of alerts contained two live, on-package, mainland-Britain leads. Not
27 a day. Several of the live ones are also Irish (Galway, Dublin, the Aran Islands),
which the keyword profile does not appear to filter.

Read the Status field before you chase anything off this feed.
