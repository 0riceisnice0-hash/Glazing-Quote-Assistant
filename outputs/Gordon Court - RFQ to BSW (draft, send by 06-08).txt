=====================================================================================
The client's targets are the design team's business, not ours. These are simply figures
we need from you in order to demonstrate compliance to them.

C1. WHOLE-WINDOW Uw. None of the four quotations states a whole-window U-value. The only
    thermal figures present are centre-pane glass values inside the make-up strings
    ("EcoPlus 1.0"). The client's energy consultant requires 1.10 W/m2K on the external
    glazing and the NBS requires a maximum of 1.2 W/m2K.
    Please state Uw for the Liniar 70 casements, the Alunet ESS47 patio doors and the
    Sheerline Prestige units.

C2. TRICKLE VENTILATION. You have quoted "4000 External Linkvent / 4000 Internal
    Linkvent" throughout. All five architect's schedules carry the note "VENTILATION:
    8000mm2 min trickle vents unless otherwise specified in window schedule", and the
    schedules do not specify a different area.
    Please price the upgrade to 8000mm2 equivalent area across the 124 windows.

C3. ACOUSTIC TRICKLE VENTS. Schedule 52002 carries an "Acoustic Trickle Vents" column
    with 26 of the 40 replacement windows ticked, and the note "If acoustic trickle
    vents required, to be Passivent AL-dB 450 or better, or similar approved". No
    acoustic vent appears on any quotation.
    Please price Passivent AL-dB 450 or equal to the marked positions. We will confirm
    the count on the new-window schedule separately.

C4. PAS 24 AND THIRD-PARTY CERTIFICATION. NBS L10 clause 330 requires the windows to
    comply "To BS6375-1, BS6375-2, BS6375-3, EN 14351-1 and Pas24", and clause 205
    requires "Independent, 3rd Party Certification Schemes" with "documentation
    confirming Certifications claimed". None of these standards is referenced on any of
    the four quotations.
    Please confirm compliance and supply the certification documentation. If any of the
    quoted configurations is not PAS 24 rated, please tell us which and price the
    difference.

C5. MANIFESTATION. NBS L20 clause 280 requires manifestation to the communal main
    entrance doors. Please price two bands to the glazed communal doors, which we
    measure as 15.002 linear metres over 5 units. For reference the range is 8.152 lm if
    only the two entrance doors are caught and 39.332 lm if every glazed external door
    is.

C6. SOLAR CONTROL ON THE OBSCURE UNITS - 23 WINDOWS.
    Nearly all of QT252247 is glazed "4Tuf/18/6.8 LAM Coolite SKN176ii Argon", which is
    solar controlled and properly specified - we have no issue with it. Three positions
    are not:
        WN_1   11no   6.8 Lam/18/4mm ObsTuff EcoPlus 1.0 Satin 4mm Black Warm Edge Sp 18mm
        WE_3   10no   as above
        WE_14   2no   as above
    These carry no Coolite and no SKN coating. All five architect's schedules carry the
    note "G-Value of 0.36 or better", and our own proposal repeats it.
    Please state the g-value of the ObsTuff make-up. If it does not achieve 0.36, please
    price an obscure unit that does - a solar-controlled obscure or a satin-etched
    Coolite - across all 23. We would rather buy the right glass now than change 23
    units later.

C7. FREE AREA OF THE SEVEN SMOKE VENTILATION UNITS - THE FIGURE WE MOST NEED.
    QT252257 states no free area for either position, and no reference to EN 12101-2.
    Please state, for each of the two units as you have quoted them:

        WN_7   3no   Prestige T&T, structural opening 910 x 2100
        WL_1   4no   Prestige Casement, your overall size 1307 x 2197

    (a) the GEOMETRIC free area in m2 that the unit achieves as quoted. Geometric,
        specifically - the specification for this job is written that way throughout
        ("1.5m2 GEOMETRIC free area", NBS L20 cl.630) and the word aerodynamic appears
        nowhere in the 186-page NBS. If you hold only an aerodynamic or Cv figure,
        please give it and label it as such rather than converting it;
    (b) whether the unit is tested to EN 12101-2, and the certificate reference;
    (c) the largest geometric free area you could achieve within a 910 x 2100 structural
        opening if we needed more.

    WHY (c) MATTERS AND WHY WE ARE ASKING YOU RATHER THAN WORKING IT OUT: the architect's
    installation note fixes ground and first floor windows to the existing structural
    openings, so at those levels the opening cannot grow. We have tried to derive the
    achievable area from the frame dimensions and it is not reliable - a 5mm change in
    the assumed section swings the answer from comfortably over to under. You hold the
    tested figure and we do not. One line from you settles it.

    We are separately asking the design team which duty these positions serve, because
    the fire strategy legend gives two different figures (1.5m2 and 0.4m2). Your answer
    and theirs together tell us whether what has been quoted complies. Neither answer is
    much use on its own.
