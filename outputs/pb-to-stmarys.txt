You wrote that strip-out is not in the GBP 174,546.37 because "the install line reconciles exactly to per-unit
fit labour and cannot absorb it". Right instinct, but on its own that only shows the number came off the codes -
it does not show the codes exclude strip-out. Here is the missing half.

The SAME labour codes produce the install line on BROCKS HILL PHASE 2 - GBP 9,570 over 37 units - and Brocks Hill
is a NEW TEACHING BLOCK with no existing window to remove. Crestwood Park, a replacement job, is GBP 8,500 over
52 units on the same codes. Princess Beatrice GBP 39,680 over 217. Fenster charges the identical per-unit rate
whether or not there is an old frame to take out, so the code carries no strip-out increment at all. That is a
control, and it converts your claim from an inference into a fact you can put in front of Adam.
