THE ONE QUESTION THAT ACTUALLY DECIDES IT

The drawing says "free area of 1m2" and does not say whether that is geometric or
aerodynamic. It matters more than the 1.0 vs 1.5 does:

  - A Plus quote GEOMETRIC free area only on QT51518: 1.30m2.
  - On their Towcester Vale quote for the same DualFrame 75Si vent they state both, and
    aerodynamic runs at 60-62% of geometric (0.49 against 0.81, and 0.54 against 0.87).
  - On that ratio our 1.30m2 geometric is roughly 0.78-0.81m2 aerodynamic - about 20% short
    of 1m2. And A Plus's proposed 1235 x 1583 "fix", which reaches 1.5m2 geometric, would be
    about 0.9m2 aerodynamic and would still miss.

So on a geometric reading we comply comfortably and nothing needs to change. On an
aerodynamic reading we do not, and neither does the bigger vent. Two things close it: A Plus
stating the aerodynamic figure for our actual sizes, and the fire strategy confirming which
basis applies. Both are worth having before we order rather than after.
