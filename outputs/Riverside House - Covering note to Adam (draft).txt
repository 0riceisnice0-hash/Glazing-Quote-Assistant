ONE THING IN A PLUS'S TERMS THAT IS WORTH A MINUTE OF YOUR TIME

Reading their conditions rather than their prices: A Plus state that it is the customer's
responsibility - ours - to ensure the Building Regulations are complied with, and that
they "do not warrant or represent that any Product supplied shall comply" unless they have
expressly said so on the quotation. Our own terms do the opposite: clause 16 says we are
not responsible for regulatory strategy and rely on the client's professional team.

Neither document is unusual and I am not suggesting either is wrong. But this product
exists to satisfy one regulation - Part B - and that is precisely the responsibility we
have disclaimed to the client and accepted from the supplier. If the 1m2 turns out to be
aerodynamic and the vent as quoted does not meet it, that gap is where the argument would
happen.

The fix is free and it is pre-order: their clause has a door in it - "unless where
expressly stated to the contrary by the Supplier" - so the letter now asks A Plus to state
the aerodynamic free area and the EN 12101-2 classification ON the revised quotation
rather than in a reply, and to confirm the whole-window U-value the same way. Asking costs
a line before an order and is a variation afterwards.

AND TWO COMMERCIAL POINTS I HAD NOT PICKED UP, BOTH LIVE

A Plus price on the basis that the materials are "ordered together, and in one phase", and
say a part order may carry extra costs and should be re-priced. We are still establishing
whether the second floor vent is a wall unit at all - if it turns out to be a roof vent we
would be ordering one of the two. So the GBP 5,990.22 should not be read as two halves that
can be taken separately; the letter now asks what a single vent would cost.

And they charge storage on goods left uncollected more than three working days after they
are ready, and exclude holding materials off-site if the programme slips - in which case they
would want paying for the materials before delivery. This is the one job in the book that is
explicitly waiting on somebody else's programme, so the gap between manufacture and a ready
opening has a price on it. Asked of A Plus, and it is now a second reason for asking RRR when
the openings will be formed.

Two smaller things from the same read: their quotation incorporates a Terms of Sale
document we have never held on any job, so the letter asks for a copy; and they assume a
1200Pa design windload and put the wind loading and fixing calculations on us, which on a
second floor elevation is work I do not believe anyone has been appointed to do. That one
has gone to RRR, since no structural engineer is named on any drawing.
