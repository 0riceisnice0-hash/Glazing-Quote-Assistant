AND THEIR "STRIP OUT" IS A LOT MORE THAN OLD FRAMES

The bill's Preparation & Stripping Out section also requires us to make good facing brickwork and pointing with matching bricks and tinted cement mortar, cut back plaster and render internally and make good with one coat gypsum dubbed out flush, and make good any internal damage at our own expense. And it makes us liable for water damage where an opening is left exposed.

Making good brickwork and plaster is builder's work, and return visits are a programme cost. Neither is fit labour. I would want strip-out written into a corrected proposal on OUR definition — removal of frames — before their sub-contract order picks up that whole section on the strength of your email.

On disposal you are right on their main clause and it is worth knowing it is not one-way. Meanwhile our own proposal excludes waste removal generally, which disclaims even the carry-to-skip they deem included.

I cannot put a number on strip-out. There is no strip-out rate anywhere in our data — it is one of the categories that returns nothing — and I am not going to invent one. The quantity for an RFQ is 217 units / 336.17 m2.
