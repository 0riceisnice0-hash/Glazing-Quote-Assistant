  While I have your attention, there are a few points on the tender I should put on record:

  - External mastic. Our pricing shows this as a separate line. Specification clause 2.33.12
    requires all joints between the aluminium and the structure to be pointed, so if you would
    prefer it carried within the tender sum rather than alongside it, the figure becomes
    GBP 90,086.00 excluding VAT.

  - Removal of existing frames. Installation and removal of the existing frames is included
    within our costs.

  - Door ironmongery. Our current allowance follows the door sizes rather than schedule 2.39.
    We are re-confirming the ironmongery against that schedule with our fabricator and will
    revert - in particular the push-bar and thumbturn split across D01 to D08, and the
    requirement for keys to be suited to the building's existing master key.

  - D02. This is quoted as a sliding patio door, which cannot accept a BS EN 1125 push-bar
    panic device. Please confirm how you would like this door treated.

  - D04. The schedule describes a double doorset at 950mm wide. We have allowed an 1800mm
    double leaf set in line with the description; please confirm which is correct.

  - Asbestos. Clause 2.43.1 places removal of the internal asbestos cill boards within the
    tender submission, while your own breakdown carries a separate asbestos element. Please
    confirm whether that removal sits with Pearce or within our package.
