2. TRIPLE GLAZING TO THE EXTERNAL DOORS

Employer's Requirements 7.5.1 and the door schedule general specification both require
factory sealed triple glazed units to windows and doors.

The windows are fine - our tender is triple glazed argon filled throughout.

The doors are not. The aluminium door system specified for this type of building is not
manufactured in triple glazing, and we have that in writing from the manufacturer. It is
not a glass substitution: no triple glazed unit is available in that frame depth.

Two ways forward and we would like your steer:

  (a) Accept a double glazed external door achieving the 1.2 W/m2K door U-value set out
      at ER 5.4.1, which the doors do meet; or
  (b) Confirm the door package is to move to a different system that is available in
      triple, which will carry a cost and a lead-time consequence.

Absent an instruction we will tender on (a) and state the deviation plainly on the face
of our quotation.


3. SOLAR CONTROL GLASS

Window schedule rev P04 marks every external window "Triple Glazed, Solar Control
Glazing" and ER 7.5.17 requires solar control glass to reduce overheating. Neither
document names a product or a g-value.

Please confirm the specification, or a target g-value, so we can price the correct
coating. Absent a figure we will tender on a named solar control product and state it,
so you can compare it like for like against the other returns.

doors and 2.2 for rooflights.

Please confirm which basis applies. It changes whether individual elements can be traded
against each other across the package.
