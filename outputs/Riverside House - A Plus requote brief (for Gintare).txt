WHY THIS IS ONE EMAIL AND NOT TWO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adam has confirmed the openings are being newly formed, so the vents can be any size
we need. The only thing still undecided is whether the drawings' 1m2 free area means
GEOMETRIC or AERODYNAMIC. Rather than wait for that answer and then ask A Plus, this
asks for both cases at once - so whichever way it falls, we already have the price.

Everything below is answerable by A Plus from their own system. They stated both free
area figures on QT51516 (Towcester Vale) three days earlier, so item 1 is not a new
piece of work for them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AERODYNAMIC FREE AREA FOR THE VENT AS QUOTED  ** the important one **

QT51518 states "Geometric free area = 1.30m2" and gives no aerodynamic figure.
Please state the APPROXIMATE AERODYNAMIC FREE AREA for the vent as quoted -
1130 x 1530, style FF, 850mm stroke single chain - in the same format used on
QT51516, which gives both on every line.

2. A SIZE THAT ACHIEVES 1.0 m2 AERODYNAMIC

If the aerodynamic figure at 1130 x 1530 is below 1.0 m2, please give the O/A size
that achieves at least 1.0 m2 AERODYNAMIC free area in the same configuration, and
price it. State BOTH the geometric and the aerodynamic figure for that size.

Please do not size it to 1.5 m2 - that figure came from our own enquiry of 24/07 and
is not what the drawings ask for. The requirement on drawings K1653-11 and K1653-12 is
1m2, once per stairwell.

3. THE ACTUATOR IF THE VENT GROWS

A Plus's own note says "the quoted prices may change if the vent sizes increase and a
larger actuator is required". If the size in item 2 needs a larger actuator or a twin
chain, please say so and price it separately so we can see what the change costs.

4. WHOLE-WINDOW U-VALUE

The drawings set a 'U' value of 1.6 against the window and door types on this building.
QT51518 quotes the glass make-up only ("4-20-4 Clr Tough S Coat 1.2"), which is a
centre-pane figure, and A Plus's standard notes say windows will be "no better than
1.8" unless otherwise stated. Please state the WHOLE-WINDOW Uw for the vent as quoted,
and confirm whether 1.6 can be met if it is required.

5. THE VENT LEAF

QT51518 lists apertures A1 (957 x 590) and A7 (957 x 591), so the frame is transom
divided, but a single 850mm stroke actuator is quoted against it. Please confirm on a
shop drawing which leaf opens, where the transom sits and where the actuator goes.

6. DELIVERY - TWO PARTS

(a) The quote is addressed to our Bradwell Abbey yard, MK13 9HF, and carries no site
    address. Please confirm delivery is to MK13 9HF and not to site.
(b) The order is GBP 4,845.22, which is GBP 154.78 below the GBP 5,000 ex VAT
    threshold for free delivery. Please confirm whether this load will be batched at
    no charge or whether carriage will be charged, and at what figure. If the resize
    in item 2 takes the order over GBP 5,000, please confirm delivery is then FOC.
    Please also quote delivery direct to site at HP19 7HL as an alternative.

7. PRICE HOLD

QT51518 is open for 30 days, to 26/08/2026. RRR are waiting on PHDB's building-works
costs before this is submitted, so the submission date is not yet known. Please confirm
how long A Plus will hold the price, or what notice we would get of a change.

NOT FOR A PLUS - FOR US
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AOV control system is not in A Plus's scope and is not in ours. The drawings
require the vent to be "OPERATED BY THE FIRE BRIGADE AT GROUND FLOOR ACCESS LEVEL IN
THE STAIRS", which needs a smoke control panel, mains and battery-backed supply,
cabling, containment, the override itself and commissioning. A Plus fix the actuator,
test it on local batteries and leave about 2m of flex coiled at the vent - that is
where they stop. Somebody needs to be carrying this, and at the moment nobody is.
It is worth settling with RRR / PHDB at the same time as the above.
