PART TWO - NOT FOR A PLUS. THESE GO TO RRR / CAMPBELL ARK / THE FIRE ENGINEER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four of these have come up since the vents were priced, and two of them could change
the answer to Part One. Worth asking before Gintare spends A Plus's time.

C1. IS THE 1m2 GEOMETRIC OR AERODYNAMIC? The drawings do not say. Our reading is
    GEOMETRIC, for two reasons: the drawings' own key works "TO AD B1", which is the
    prescriptive Approved Document B route and states stair vents as a free area,
    where aerodynamic is the language of the engineered BS 9991 / EN 12101-2 route;
    and comparable packs specify this exact duty as "1m2 GEOMETRIC free area" in so
    many words. On that reading the vents as quoted clear the requirement by 30% and
    nothing needs to change. Please confirm.

C2. ARE THE OPENINGS REALLY NEW? We have been told the openings are being newly
    formed and can therefore be made any size. **The pack does not show that.**
    Drawing K1653-04 "EXISTING / PROPOSED ELEVATIONS" carries a single set of eight
    elevations showing a complete, regularly fenestrated building, with no new opening
    marked anywhere and no AOV drawn on any elevation. If the vents are in fact going
    into EXISTING openings, then enlarging them is structural work - new lintels,
    cutting masonry, making good - none of which is in anybody's price, and it would
    also change the approved external appearance. Please confirm per vent.

C3. AND IF THEY ARE ENLARGED, DOES THAT NEED PLANNING? The drawings are stamped
    24/02303/PAPCR, which is a PRIOR APPROVAL reference rather than a full planning
    permission. Prior-approval conversions normally carry tight limits on external
    alterations. Before anyone commits to "make them as big as we need", please
    confirm with HD Planning whether enlarging these two openings sits inside the
    approval or needs a separate application.

C4. WHICH FACE DOES THE SECOND-FLOOR VENT SIT IN, AND IS THE HEAD ARCHED? The
    second-floor stairwell is in the central tower. On Elevation F that tower's top
    storey has TWO ARCHED-HEAD windows; on Elevation B it has three rectangular ones.
    A Plus have quoted a square-head 1130 x 1530 casement. If the vent lands in an
    arched opening it is a different unit at a different price. Please confirm the
    face and the head shape.

C5. ARE K1653-11 AND K1653-12 STILL CURRENT? They are the only documents that carry
    the smoke-vent requirement, and neither has ever been revised - both are dated
    Mar 24 with no revision. The ground floor plan, K1653-10b, has been revised twice
    since ("Dental Lobby added" and "altered", both Nov 25). A requirement sitting
    only on the sheets that never moved, in a pack where another sheet moved a year
    later, is worth one line of confirmation.

C6. WHO IS CARRYING THE AOV CONTROL SYSTEM? It is not in A Plus's scope and it is not
    in ours. The drawings require the vent to be "OPERATED BY THE FIRE BRIGADE AT
    GROUND FLOOR ACCESS LEVEL IN THE STAIRS", which needs a smoke control panel, mains
    and battery-backed supply, cabling, containment, the override itself and
    commissioning. A Plus fix the actuator, test it on local batteries and leave about
    2m of flex coiled at the vent - that is where they stop. Worth settling with RRR /
    PHDB at the same time as the above.
