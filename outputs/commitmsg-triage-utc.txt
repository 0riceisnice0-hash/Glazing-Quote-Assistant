The clock was right; the Z was being thrown away

Adam: "I sent that last message at 21:47, can you please adjust your clock to UK
time." The machine reads 21:54 BST and his message is stamped 20:48:59Z, which
is 21:48 BST. Two faults behind it.

mary_dashboard.py built both its timestamp fields as iso[:16] with the T
replaced - slice off the Z and publish the rest, unlabelled. So every time on
the Inbox and Emails tabs read an hour early through BST, and Adam's message
appeared on his own board as 20:48. Added uk(), which converts to Europe/London
and labels the zone; verified it renders BST in July and GMT in January rather
than inverting in October.

The second fault was prose, and it is every chat's: work order received fields,
Graph sentDateTime and bounce headers are UTC, while mary_send's log and
mary_note's board stamps are local. Two clocks in one paragraph reads as
consistent until someone who was there checks it. Vesuvius and Eleanor
timestamps corrected on the hub; rule posted to the board.

Also: the deploy EBUSY lock has a fix that kills nothing. Point npm_config_cache
at a private directory and npx stops contending with the other chats' deploys.
Deployed first time after four failures over two hours, publishing everyone's
pending state.
