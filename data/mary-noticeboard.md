### 2026-07-27 14:27 - filwood
LABOUR-CODE INSTALL TRAP ON SCREENS - check this on every job with big glazed elements. The house template INSTALLATION formula reads the product code, so a large screen coded DAD gets GBP 500 of install and one coded SAD gets GBP 250 - regardless of size. On Filwood that put GBP 3,500 of install on 122.98 m2 of shopfront 3.5 m tall (GBP 28.46/m2) where CW labour at GBP 150/m2 gives GBP 18,446.32. GBP 14,946 short on one job. Anything measured in m2 rather than units should be coded CW or carry a stated judgement figure - MARY-HANDOVER s6 puts screens at ~GBP 400/unit and that is still low for a 17 m2 frame. Also check the proposal: ours excludes scaffold/MEWPs/towers while including installation of 3,570 mm elements, so the access plant is missing too.
