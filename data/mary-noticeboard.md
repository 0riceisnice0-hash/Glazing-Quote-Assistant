### 2026-07-27 14:51 - triage
READ THE REVISION DATE, NOT THE DATE THE ADDENDUM ARRIVED. On St Mary's, ET&S issued "revised drawings" on 24/07. The window schedule inside was revision A dated 13.07.26 and the site plan revision E dated 08.07.26 - both revised BEFORE Fenster quoted on 17/07. So we priced a superseded drawing for nine days without knowing. It cost nothing that time because the change was an omission we had already excluded, but the same gap could as easily hide an addition. When an addendum lands: check every revision date against the date of our own quote, and say so plainly if we were pricing stale information.

HOW TO COMPARE TWO REVISIONS OF A SCHEDULE. A line-by-line text diff of two PDFs is unreliable - pdfplumber tokenises the same drawing differently between revisions (on St Mary's the same floor plan came out as 937 lines in one revision and 3,285 in the other, which produces pages of fake differences). Compare COUNTS OF THE THINGS THAT DRIVE PRICE instead: window references, type codes, structural opening sizes, opening patterns, restrictor notes, U-value notes. Identical counts across all of them is a real answer; a clean text diff is not. And always read the architect's own revision note in the title block - on St Mary's it said "integral blind omitted" and was the whole story.

ALSO: a revision note can be wrong. St Mary's rev A said the integral blind was omitted, but one of the 29 blind notes survived the deletion - sitting on the most expensive window type on the job. Check that a stated omission actually happened everywhere before relying on it.

A SYSTEMATIC SHORTFALL IS USUALLY A CATEGORY, NOT AN ERROR. On Stoke Park I raised the alarm that the glass order was 46 panes short of Aplus's final list. Adam's answer: they are louvres, glazed in in place of glass, from IKON not Vetroseal. He was right, and the proof was already in the job folder - Aplus panel order 'QT50932 Rev7 Louvres.pdf' (46 panels) and IKON quote Q26-24329 of 02/07 (46 IKL332 modules, GBP 10,125.91). Take them out and the glass reconciles exactly, 124 = 124. The tell I should have read: the gap was one pane per bay on six window types and one whole door - that regularity means a category the schedule deliberately excludes, not a clerical miss. Before flagging a shortfall, search the job folder for the OTHER supplier who covers it (louvres, spandrel panels, infills, brise soleil). Fenster's price often splits one elevation across two suppliers.
