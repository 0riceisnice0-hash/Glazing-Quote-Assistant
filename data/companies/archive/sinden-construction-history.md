## Why the board had this client the wrong way round (30/07/2026)

AdminBase lead 5493 read "Live - Quoted, 523 days silent" and generated the next action "chase
Sinden for a final answer on The Hub Alkerden". Sinden were never silent: they wrote to
commercial@ on 01/07/2026 asking **us** for an updated quotation. Ringing to ask whether the job
was still live would have been asking a client who had already told us it was secured, given us
the programme, and been waiting on a price.

**The general rule it belongs to: establish who is actually waiting on whom before drafting
either the chase or the apology.** Both were half true here and the halves pointed opposite ways.

and health facility - lifelong learning centre and hall, ground floor plus three storeys, parking,
landscaping. Package: Aluminium Curtain Walling & External Doors, Stage 4.

  please confirm the security rating for the external doorsets? And the curtain walling?" Paul

## OSG Cold Ash - the planning evidence

Former St Gabriel's Convent, The Ridge, Cold Ash, Thatcham RG18 9HU. OSG is **OneSchool Global**.
Emma O'Brien told estimating@ on 26/06/2026 the project was "on hold due to a planning issue";
Adam replied 27/06 "I will update our notes accordingly".

West Berkshire's register says it harder than the client did. Application **25/01899/FULMAJ** -
change of use of the convent site to an educational facility, demolition of the convent, retreat
building and chapel, a new two-storey teaching building with single storey sports hall,
reconfigured parking for OneSchool Global, plus access works for St Finian's Catholic Primary.
Validated 12/09/2025, target decision 07/11/2025, **REFUSED, decision issued 21 May 2026**. No
appeal and no resubmission recorded at that postcode as of 30/07/2026.

`https://publicaccess.westberks.gov.uk/online-applications/applicationDetails.do?keyVal=T1A14ARDLO200&activeTab=summary`

**PlanIt had this as "Undecided / Awaiting decision"** - its record was last scraped 20/09/2025,
ten months stale. The council's own page was the only one that was right.
