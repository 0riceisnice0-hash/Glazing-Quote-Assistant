## BD angle - why this file is worth keeping

The pane is small work, and that is the argument for it, not against it. Fenster's funnel wins 38%
under £10k and 0% over £50k, and its best clients are repeat reactive ones - FM Solutions 10 won /
2 lost, Cranfield 7-0.

**A national hoarding contractor is a channel, not a customer.** Excel put hoardings up around
refurbishments, shopfitting and damage across the country; broken shopfront glass turns up on those
same sites constantly and it is not work they self-deliver. They came to us cold, by phone, for one
pane - which means somebody gave them our name, or found us, and there was no incumbent glazier.
That gap is the opportunity.

They are Bradford-based and this job is Solihull, so geography is clearly not their constraint.

**The way to convert them is to deliver this one well and be right about the glass** - including
being the supplier who said "hold on, the spec may be wrong" rather than the one who took the bigger
order. Then one sentence to Gareth: who do we speak to about being on your list for glazing. Not
before the unit is in.
