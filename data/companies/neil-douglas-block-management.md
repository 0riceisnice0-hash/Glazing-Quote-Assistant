## Why this one matters more than its value

A block and estate manager, so - like Glazing Consultancy Services - they are not the payer
and not the main contractor. They put contractors in front of leaseholder-funded works.
The relationship buys the thing that is actually scarce in this trade: **being asked to
price.** Five Fenster quotes sit on this one account.

**We have just won the first of them.** Anton, 29/06, unprompted:

> "I am looking forward to getting one formal project carried out with your team. Let's
> make it a good one and hopefully more to follow!"

Earleswood Court is GBP 14,003 ex VAT - the band where Fenster actually converts, not the
GBP 50k+ band where the log is 52-0. Delivering it cleanly is the lead generator here, and
it is the whole of the next three months' BD value on this account.

## Section 20 is why these quotes sit for months

Leaseholder-funded works run through statutory consultation before anyone can be
instructed. Earleswood Court needed a **second notice of consultation, 20 days**, and Anton
could not issue a works order until it expired on 30/07. He said so on 29/06 and then did
exactly what he said on the day it lapsed.

**So on this account, silence is process, not disinterest.** The board had these rows at
"173 / 188 / 426 days silent" with a generic chase attached to each. Every one of those
chases would have been wrong. Do not read elapsed days as a signal here without asking what
stage of consultation the block is at.

**The failure here is ours and it is a new kind.** On Anton's blocks silence is process. On
this one, Jordan asked a direct question on 27/07 - *is this something you will be able to
quote for* - and offered to meet on site, and **eight days later nothing had left any Fenster
mailbox to him.** The entire trail is info@ to estimating@ to Adam to Mary. So the account
now has two distinct failure modes on record: an outcome arriving by email that nobody
carries to the CRM (Anton, 29/06), and **an invitation arriving by email that nobody
answers.** Both are internal. Neither is the client.

**Price it from a site visit, not from photographs.** The pack carries no door sizes. ITT
1.4.1 says the tenderer should visit before tendering; 3.1.2 bars claims for extras for
anything a visit would reasonably have revealed. Pricing off the photos means carrying all
five openings' measurement risk on a fixed price held open 90 days. Jordan offered the walk
round for free.

**The tender is not just a number,** and the non-price boxes are not estimating's: itemised
pricing carried to a collection summary, a separate hourly trade rates sheet that becomes
contractual and prices all variations, names/addresses/trades of every subcontractor,
estimated duration, proposed commencement, installation and manufacturer warranties, public
liability insurance, signed Form of Tender Acceptance.
