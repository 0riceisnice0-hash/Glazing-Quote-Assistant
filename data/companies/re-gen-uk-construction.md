## Why the windows were never winnable as priced

**Wandsworth's own Replacement Window & Door Performance Specification (Rev D, Sept 2020)
was in the pack we received on 15/05/2025**, at Appendix J, page 214 of 293. Clause 2.6
names the five permitted profiles: **Rehau, VEKA, Kommerling (profine), Schueco,
Deceuninck**. Liniar is not among them. The **original** priced Liniar anyway and we found out
eight months later, on the phone, from the client.

**But the reprice FIXED that, and nobody recorded it.** Mary, 30/07 (msg 39): Ashley Walton at
Titan confirmed in writing on 24/02 14:24 that *"the quotation we provided was in the REHAU TOTAL
70 62MM OUTER FRAME"*, with three Rehau Total70 data sheets attached - and **Rehau is first on
clause 2.6's list.** Those sheets went to Danny at 14:42 the same day, so **Re-Gen hold our
profile evidence. REV 1 was on-list**, and it was not price-and-hope: the windows lost on money,
not on compliance.

**What IS still missing is clause 2.1, and it is a different animal.** That clause wants
licensed-fabricator evidence submitted to the **Council** - and Titan are the Rehau fabricator,
not Fenster. Nothing has ever gone to Wandsworth or to HJP. **A missing submission, not a wrong
specification** - which strengthens the HJP action below rather than weakening it.

Clause 2.1 is the sharper one: *"Evidence of their current status as licensed fabricators
and installers shall be submitted by all companies at the time of tendering, and any
company not considered by the Council to be appropriately registered shall not have their
tender considered."* Plus PAS 24:2016, BS 7412 Kitemark, BS EN ISO 9001:2015, Secured by
Design.

**So the Council vets the window subcontractor, not Re-Gen.** That is the mechanism behind
Barham Park: Re-Gen won the main contract and *"the client has chosen their own window
contractor - Liam suggests a brown envelope has been handed over"* (BD log, 21/11/2025).
Our client winning is not step two of the job when the employer holds the approved list.

**Closed, and it was the right question to ask:** REV 1's profile was Rehau, confirmed in writing
by the supplier on the day of issue. **"Non-compliant before anyone read the number" was my fear
and it is not true** - take it as withdrawn.

**And the fire egress caveat is on the issued document, which is the check that mattered most.**
Bedford Trade Glass told us on 30/01 that none of the designs meet fire escape; Jayk priced without
egress and the issued cover sheet says so - *"Some of the windows does not meet the Fire egress
requirements. Design would need to be adjusted."* 852 windows with no escape openers and no caveat
would have been a live liability. It is there.

**THE OPEN LOOP THAT COST THE WINDOWS, AND IT WAS INTERNAL.** Adam, 24/02 12:14, before the quote
