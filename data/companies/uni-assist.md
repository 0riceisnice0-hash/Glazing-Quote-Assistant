## The job

**Walter Tull House, Northampton NN1 1NS.** Whole building, **~50 aluminium windows.**

Mark's own diagnosis, 20/07: a lot of the windows have simply been **sealed with silicone**,
there is significant draught internally and externally, and handles and opening mechanisms
are worn. Uni Assist attended to repair and concluded they needed a professional survey
instead. **No historic information exists for the building.**

Required report, in PDF: full condition survey of all window elements; marked-up layout
drawings and window locations; defects, deterioration, operational issues, water ingress risk,
failed components and safety concerns; photographic records; remedial recommendations split
short / medium / long term; prioritisation by condition, risk and urgency; budget costs for
repair, refurbishment and replacement; and a summary schedule of window reference, defect,
recommended action, priority rating and indicative cost.

**Site plans are NOT a blocker.** Uni Assist have asked the end client for them and are
awaiting a response, but Mark settled this on 28/07: "As long as we have a full report that
highlights all my request apart from the plans then this should be fine." Do not let
"waiting on plans" become a reason the survey slips - producing the marked-up drawings is
part of what they are paying us for.
