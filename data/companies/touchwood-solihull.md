## The job, and the technical knot that is holding it

One shopfront unit, **2690 x 3430mm** - a big pane, and every problem here comes from its size.

- **Page 10 of the O&M we were issued** states all glass units are to be **toughened laminated**.
  Gareth Siddle at Excel Hoardings pointed us at it on 30/07.
- **Our surveyor gauged the existing glass at ~10.8mm**, which normally means plain laminated, not
  toughened laminated. So either the O&M was never followed on this unit, or the gauge is wrong.
- **A toughened laminated unit at this size comes out ~17.5mm.** Paul told Steve on 03/08 that
  laminated "is the only option that would allow us to guarantee that the replacement glass will
  fit within the existing frame."
- **The size also cuts down the supply chain.** Euroview ruled themselves out on 30/07 - their
  maximum for toughened laminate is 2500 x 4500 and this unit is 2690 wide - unless the unit is
  split in half. Specialist Glass Products priced a 17.5mm toughened laminate with polished edges;
  CN Glass priced the 10.8mm laminated option. **Numbers are in the commercial@ thread and are
  Gintare's and Mary's, not mine.**

**Both answers cost money, in opposite directions.** If Steve confirms the original unit was plain
laminated, this morning's increase should come back off and we look good for having asked. If he
insists on toughened laminated, there is a frame-fit problem to solve before anything is ordered,
and the supply options narrow to those who can make 2690 wide.
