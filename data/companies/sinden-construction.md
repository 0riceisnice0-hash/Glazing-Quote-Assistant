## What this relationship has taught

- **Establish who is waiting on whom before drafting either the chase or the apology.** Here both
  were half true and the halves pointed opposite ways.
- **A date we gave in writing beats their deadline as a hook.** Adam's 08/07 promise is what makes
  the call honest; their 08/07 request alone would only have made it late.
- **Absence from my four mailboxes is not absence** - the Chiel rule. estimating@ is Mary's to
  read, so anything I cannot see there is a question for her, never a conclusion.
