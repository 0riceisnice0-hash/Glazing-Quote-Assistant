# What counts as noise in this inbox

Written by the triage pass and by whoever corrects it. One line each. This file
is the only memory the front door has, and it is loaded on every classification -
so keep it short and keep it true.

- Supply2Gov / In-Tend / OnceForAll portal digests and daily opportunity alerts.
  The real invitations arrive as ordinary mail from a person, not as a digest.
- "Meet the buyers" and similar event marketing from procurement portals.
- In-Procurement Magazine and other trade advertising.
- Hightown Housing RFQs: Adam closed the client on 27/07 - "please disregard
  their quotes unless instructed otherwise". Their portal does not know, and
  keeps sending. An instruction FROM Adam about them is never noise.

Not noise, however automated it looks:
- A supplier order acknowledgement. It is the evidence a step is done.
- Anything from adam@, zac@ or marketing@. Ever.
