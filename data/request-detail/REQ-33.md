# REQ-33 - Grange Hill Methodist Church: spec 3.16 and 3.15.2

Raised 28/07/2026. Full text as first written, archived 29/07 when the request was cut down to
something Adam would actually read. Nothing here is lost from the short version - it is the working,
not the question.

## The question

Is spec 3.16 in Fenster's package, and is 3.15.2?

## Why it arises

Our package is **WD001 Windows and Doors**, and the specification's door clauses run **3.11 to 3.16
without a break**. Zac ruled on 27/07 that 3.15, the chapel folding doors, is ours.

**3.16 sits immediately after it and has never been priced, never been in any RFQ and never been
raised with anyone:**

- **3.16.1** - remove the existing fire check door to the corridor including its frame, clear from
  site, set aside the timber for the client.
- **3.16.2** - supply **2No FD60 doorsets, 1200mm wide**, with oblong vision panes (150 x 760mm and
  150 x 450mm), adjustable overhead closers, 75 x 300mm aluminium finger plates, Briton 4700 D pull
  handles, a mortise lock with thumbturn to the nursery/hall door, and **magnetic hold-opens wired
  into the fire detection system** with manual release. Plus timber infill framing around the frame,
  two layers of 12mm plasterboard both sides, gaps filled with fire-rated foam, joints taped and the
  whole surface skimmed. The bell push refitted to the nursery/hall door.

Parts of 3.16 are **zero-rated for VAT** - the spec says so twice: the opening is being enlarged to
provide disabled access, and the magnetic hold-opens are fitted for disabled access.

Separately, **3.15.2** - the horizontal frosted strip patterned privacy film across the folding
doors, full width and 1.2m high - is the one part of 3.15 that never reached BSW. Gintare's second
RFQ of 24/07 15:29 carried 3.15.1 almost verbatim but not the film.

## Why we cannot settle it ourselves

The work package description behind **"View enquiry"** on the Once For All portal **has never been
opened**. The Document Register issued 24/07 09:20 lists 26 drawings and the main-contract
specification - no form of tender, no package conditions. So the only definition of our scope we
hold is the words "Windows and Doors" on the package title.

The specification is the **whole main contract** (JCT MW): strip-out, foundations, drainage, steel,
walls, floors, roofs, then the doors block, then electrics, heating, decoration, paving. Fenster
prices a slice of it and nobody has written down where the slice starts and stops.

## What follows either way

If 3.16 is ours it needs pricing from scratch. It is joinery, plasterwork and a fire-alarm
connection - **BSW cannot quote it**, so it needs a different supplier and it will not arrive
quickly.

If it is not ours it needs **excluding in writing on the return**. Neither item is priced and neither
is excluded, and a silent gap reads as included to the client.
